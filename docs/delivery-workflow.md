## Playable delivery first — owner decision, 4 October 2026

**Enforcing-stage precedence:** Before P0-035, deliver existing parts and existing puzzles as small playable development slices. This owner decision supersedes earlier blanket "required now", "every increment", profile, prerequisite, audit and SnapshotApproval wording where it would require the deferred qualification below before playable acceptance or development publication. Requirements and IDs are retained; their enforcing stage changes. A scoped playable Pass is never complete element/engine/device qualification.

**Required for each playable slice:** the actual intended interaction in Chrome through UI controls, meaningful affected negative/control cases, typed declarations and admission, generic WASM SIMD f32 physical laws and shared C# animation, affected production build, ordinary Run/Pause/Reset and supported Save/Load correctness. Preserve atomic rejection, canonical restoration and basic ownership/command/event integrity. Crashes, deadlocks, data loss, incorrect physical/game outcomes and obviously unplayable responsiveness remain blocking; fix their smallest demonstrated cause. Settle only the physical/model decisions necessary for this interaction. Numerical tolerances follow the [game-grade envelope](gpu-f32-physics.md#game-grade-envelope): residuals clamp or continue, and residual-based rejection of a tick is not an acceptance criterion. A reproduced shared contact failure that prevents ordinary play is a correctness blocker now, not deferred performance work; resolve its finite model/oracle with the same pair. Deeper analysis needs a concrete current decision or reproduced blocker, not a hypothetical future cost. Do not replace a missing shared capability with a per-element solver or animation loop.

**Later qualification, not playable blockers:** matched historical performance baselines, detailed timing/allocation/compiler/task attribution, optimization experiments, device matrices, injected loss/stall/fault campaigns, exhaustive retention/resource stress and repository-wide audit qualification (including global CHECK-* and additive ELEMENT/UI/CONTENT/PERF profile stress, exhaustive cadence and injected-delay matrices). Scoped schema/admission correctness remains required. P0-032 owns full worker/fault qualification; P0-034 owns unchanged performance/device/retention budgets and optimization; P0-030/031/035 own complete consumer/coverage/cleanup audits and engine closure. Source-specific owners remain accountable within those gates. Keep available measurements and known Fail/Incomplete results visible; do not create a collector, reopen unchanged historical evidence or run a global audit merely to publish the next playable slice. A concrete ordinary-use defect still requires its affected local check.

The measured roughly 3 ms whole-tick p95 exceeds the unchanged 2.5 ms target: retain that Fail under P0-034. Missing matched non-regression remains Incomplete there. Neither blocks Receiver playable acceptance/publication or the next existing interaction. No supported-device, performance or full release Pass is implied. All applicable deferred requirements must pass before P0-035; subsequent supported-release changes preserve the qualified envelope.

**Investigative UI is not acceptance:** try the current construction through real controls as soon as it can produce useful evidence, even while numerical findings remain open. A successful route does not erase an applicable failed contact/whole-run control. Keep one gameplay slice and pair through its fixes: each experiment names the reproduced failure, smallest causal shared-law change and discriminating check. Return to the complete intended interaction and affected controls after a causal result; do not create a new task for each microfix or repeat unchanged expression variants. Release the slice only when its required-now correctness, actual UI/build/lifecycle and independent publication checks pass. An independent next interaction may proceed only after its actual capability dependencies are reviewed, not to hide this failure.

**Review and publication:** name the current playable criteria and deferred owners in the existing slice record. SnapshotApproval and terminal review enforce those current criteria and actual artifact/remote identities plus applicable deployed gameplay checks, not the deferred profiling/fault/global-audit package. Preserve original failed evidence and distinct verdicts. Use concise scoped evidence, source/link/preservation checks for documentation, and local affected correctness checks; no new evidence platform.

**Ordered sequence:** follow the current outcome, blocker and next interaction in [TODO](../TODO.md); completed examples and older plans do not restart the queue. The full source acceptance of each part/fixture remains open until its remaining modes and qualification pass. Extend the general engine only as needed by the next interaction; preserve the [inexpensive optimization boundaries](general-engine-design.md#optimization-options) without speculative infrastructure. Broad analysis and tuning follow working gameplay at their named gates.

Forward-only changes, enum typing, evidence integrity and palette preservation apply immediately. Completing a gate never retires a requirement.** No plan, checkbox, average FPS counter or unchanged historical pass establishes current compliance.

### Focused agentic engineering and reusable role prompts

These rules adapt the four principles in the [Karpathy-inspired community guidance](https://github.com/multica-ai/andrej-karpathy-skills) and its [agent instructions](https://github.com/multica-ai/andrej-karpathy-skills/blob/main/CLAUDE.md). This is a community synthesis, not an official specification authored by Karpathy. The cited original post was inaccessible during this review; no direct verification of that post is claimed. The delivery cadence, retained review pair and progress triggers below are project-specific rules.

- **Make uncertainty explicit.** State assumptions that could change behavior or acceptance, inspect the current owner and callers, and identify material alternatives before coding. Resolve routine choices from current contracts. Ask the user only for a consequential unresolved decision; continue independent authorised work.
- **Choose the simplest complete design.** Implement the required behavior with existing patterns. Add abstractions, configurability or infrastructure only for a current requirement or demonstrated dependency. Simplicity cannot remove required cadence controls, rejection, rollback, lifecycle safety or numerical precision.
- **Keep changes surgical.** Every changed line must serve the selected outcome, its necessary caller/content cutover, verification or documentation. Avoid adjacent cleanup and speculative optimisation. Surgical scope includes the entire affected dependency closure: it cannot leave a retired path, incompatible caller or partial forward migration behind.
- **Define observable success first.** Translate the outcome into independent expected results and named positive/control/boundary checks before implementation. Use a short sequence of action → discriminating check. Investigate a failure with a concrete hypothesis; do not tune the oracle to the observed output. Reuse applicable proof under the existing identity policy.

Use these short role prompts with the slice's existing record and exact source criteria; do not paste the whole backlog or duplicate contracts into each assignment:

| Role | Assignment |
| --- | --- |
| Coordinator | Name the current observable outcome, existing IDs, genuine dependencies, allowed scope, required-now checks and deferred enforcing gates. Resolve conflicting current guidance before dependent work. Retain the pair, make scope decisions and maintain current/next ready outcomes. |
| Implementation owner | State material assumptions and the smallest complete design. Implement one outcome and its affected callers/content; run targeted checks and return exact changes, actual results, unresolved risks and the next check. Retain ownership through fixes. |
| Independent reviewer | Read the actual diff and source criteria, derive independent expectations and challenge affected direct/transitive behavior. Map each blocking finding to a criterion, failure/risk, reason it blocks and smallest resolving check. Independently re-review fixes; preserve the required identity, impact matrix, evidence and verdict. |

**Backlog progress:** When the user authorises the full TODO-linked goal, keep only the current slice and the next one or two ready outcomes in the working sequence. After independent acceptance and required publication, advance without routine reconfirmation. Honour engine-first, technical prerequisites and individual element publication; an intermediate runnable checkpoint cannot close the complete contract. For a narrower user request, stop at its authorised boundary.

**Stagnation trigger:** After roughly 45 minutes without newly runnable behavior or a resolved concrete blocker, or two failed attempts at the same setup/check, the coordinator briefly reassesses the current hypothesis, acceptance criterion and next experiment. For documentation/design slices, use a concrete reviewable decision or correction as progress. Continue a longer investigation only with a stated reason and new discriminating evidence; otherwise narrow the experiment or advance genuinely independent authorised work. Record a blocker with owner and retry condition when external change is needed. This is a replanning trigger, never a time limit on required proof or permission to waive a gate.

**Bound supporting work:** Before expanding instrumentation, evidence collection or research, identify the acceptance question and how the result changes the next decision. Smoke-test collection before expensive runs. Reuse existing records and original raw evidence; report runtime delivery separately from tests, plans and captures. Preserve failed attempts and required global audits; unrelated findings retain their owners, while unknown affected coverage remains Incomplete. These rules refine execution within the paired workflow and stage gates above; they do not replace any criterion.

### Evidence identity and hashing

Capture identities once for the scoped frozen candidate, including relevant additions, deletions and generated/untracked inputs. Reuse existing immutable Git/blob or build-manifest identities when they establish the exact bytes; hash only relevant uncommitted inputs, scoped diffs and new artifacts whose identity is not already established. A path or timestamp alone is not byte identity. Reference original evidence instead of copying manifests or hashing reports into their own approval.

The independent reviewer validates identity and applicability when accepting or reusing proof.
