# General data-driven engine design

This is the required architecture for the entire planned game, not a claim that all models are frozen or implemented. It implements the [general-engine requirement](engine-contracts.md#general-data-driven-engines) using [canonical Half/WGSL f16 values](gpu-f16-physics.md), existing [capability identities](coverage/engine-capabilities.json), [source acceptance](planning/requirements.md) and [stage gates](delivery-workflow.md#stage-gates). The [element map](planning/general-engine-element-map.md) assigns all 72 current catalogue identities and 293 retained EL/TH/RAD/GAP source identities to candidate capability compositions and their existing owners. Every named variant and the 302 authored fixtures remain separately required; potential and umbrella entries retain their source disposition.

Three different states must remain visible: **architectural route** (the records and execution path below can express the requirement), **frozen model** (the particular law, parameters, discrete semantics and independent oracle have passed their required design stage), and **qualified support** (implementation and all applicable runtime proof have passed). A capability name or table row establishes neither of the latter two. An unresolved route/model rejects admission and blocks that source's implementation gate; it does not authorize a custom part solver.

## Preserve optimization options without tuning ahead

Implement the current interaction straightforwardly in the intended C#/WASM workers, WGSL f16 physics and Chrome presentation. "Native capability" here means what that intended implementation can already do, not a CPU physics fallback, another backend or qualification from native-only tests. Keep these inexpensive boundaries in the affected code; they do not require new infrastructure or an all-engine redesign:

- Semantic IDs, canonical data and observable ordering stay separate from private indices, buffer packing and dispatch layout. Select shared capabilities from typed declarations, never catalogue identity. Preserve batch-capable typed ranges/contiguous storage boundaries; public contracts must not require one object allocation, dispatch, readback or message per puzzle element. Choose simple private layouts now; later changes still preserve numerical and event semantics.
- Workers retain exclusive ownership and bounded asynchronous queues/buffers. Physical time follows the declared master-clock projection, independent of rendering cadence; animation cannot become a physics scheduler. Do not bake the development device's speed, workgroup choice or display rate into gameplay or public admission semantics.
- Reuse compiled static topology while its declared revision and inputs remain valid; invalidate on relevant changes. This is ordinary plan ownership, not a requirement to add a speculative cache. Resource creation, reuse and disposal have explicit owners and deterministic lifecycle boundaries; no externally retained view may outlive its storage.
- Separate correctness contracts (laws, canonical values, ordering, atomicity and observable results) from execution strategy (packing, batching, scheduling and admissible algorithms). Future tuning must pass those contracts; preserving the option does not justify implementing an optimization now.

Use the [playable-first phase policy](delivery-workflow.md#playable-first): resolve a concrete current gameplay defect and its bounded shared-law oracle now. Defer exhaustive analysis, instrumentation and tuning until representative working scenes reveal the actual cost, under the [performance plan](browser-physics-performance.md#budgets-and-measurement-contract). No new per-slice benchmark gate follows from these boundaries.

## Evolve capabilities through playable outcomes

Use the [rolling playable roadmap](planning/invest/vertical-delivery.md#rolling-playable-roadmap) to turn this architecture into a sequence of current game interactions. A catalogue composition describes possible needs; the admitted implementation and applicable independent proof determine what can run. Add a missing shared capability only with its consuming outcome, including actual animation/presentation bindings and restoration—not a physics-only feature with feedback postponed.

For example, detector passage adds WGSL crossing/clearance observations and a reusable discrete rearm policy; Delay adds a shared deadline state machine and a countdown definition; the existing delayed-signal level adds a generic occurrence-time goal. Continuous physical predicates remain WGSL; integer scheduling and Boolean/event transitions remain C#. The Animation worker consumes committed state/events using the existing evaluator and occurrence lifecycle. A new timer cannot become a part-local update loop, and an animated countdown cannot become time authority.

Keep shape admission, physical laws, controller/goal operations and animation kinds independently typed, but compile and validate their complete required composition for each selected interaction. Existing declared records/evaluators may suffice; do not first build a universal graph editor, expression language or all-domain kernel library. Unsupported compositions reject atomically. A future optimization can change private packing/batching within the correctness contract; it cannot change the source outcome or bypass genuine model decisions.

## Data, definitions and ownership

Catalogue assemblies contain values and references. They cannot carry executable expressions, delegates, per-part callbacks or a catalogue identity that selects an equation. Closed alternatives use enums; extensible definitions and instances use typed stable IDs. Definition lookup resolves an authored composition before execution. The resulting engine plan contains capability/shape/material kinds and instance handles, not a switch over Basketball, Receiver or any other catalogue name.

These are semantic record families to implement through the existing compiler and ABI owners, not a new wire schema or an instruction to build an ECS. Exact admitted fields, offsets, enum mappings and quotas must pass P0-004/P0-008/P0-016 before use.

| Record family | Required contents | Execution owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Assembly definition and construction instance | Definition/version identity, stable member keys, initial transforms, canonical Half parameters and units/scales, component membership, exposed typed ports, inventory and supported modes | C# construction compiler; no physical evolution |
| Body, collider and material | Motion kind, mass/inertia, pose/velocity, compound geometry/feature identities, material/constitutive kind and coefficients, participation flags and bounds | Shared WGSL f16 dynamics, geometry and contact |
| Constraint, load and transfer port | Body/frame endpoints, allowed degrees of freedom, axes/limits, elastic/damping data, finite effort/work, source/store IDs and work-conjugate couplings | Shared WGSL constraint and domain kernels |
| Material inventory and field | Species/mass, energy/enthalpy, supported phase/constitutive data, thermal/fluid/electrical/optical/acoustic/radiation ports, geometry and explicit environment boundaries | Shared WGSL numerical models |
| Sensor definition | Typed observable/query kind, target bodies/regions/reference frames, material/channel filters, threshold/hysteresis, continuous residence or accumulated exposure policy, units and capacity | WGSL numerical predicates/integrals; committed typed outputs |
| Controller definition | Finite enum states, typed Boolean/enum/event tests, integer tick/deadline operations, transition priority and typed actuator intents | Reusable C# discrete state-machine operations; no physical formula |
| Goal definition | Typed quantity/rate/window/order/protected-state predicate and target identities; exact occurrence and completion rules | WGSL numerical observations and reusable C# discrete goal orchestration |
| Animation definition | Shared evaluator kind, typed curve/track parameters, start/end/duration/repeat, clock projection, feedback/event bindings, overlap/visibility/removal policy | Separate general C# Animation worker |
| Presentation binding | Typed target/property, immutable descriptor, baseline, physical pose or animation instance IDs, declarative mapping and art/resource identities | Browser adapter; final property application only |

All floating game data remains canonical Half with declared scales/cells. IDs, enum tags, counts and integer scheduling retain their correct types. Rendering/platform widening stays at explicit adapters. A source's authored formula such as a speed gain is represented by a supported reusable law and typed coefficients; arbitrary serialized mathematical code is not an escape hatch.

Bodies, inventories, sensors, controller state and animation instances each have one owner. Cross-domain references share typed identities and revisions, not mirrored mutable authority. A physical shaft is a body/constraint; decorative spin is a separate cosmetic track. The distinction depends on functional geometry/work, not the appearance or name of the part.

## Compile and admit one complete candidate

1. Decode the complete current construction, definitions, mode values, ports and resource references. Validate enum variants, finite canonical values, units, ranges, identities and required fields before mutation. Do not supply an old schema or silently simplify an unsupported model.
2. Expand assembly members deterministically and allocate their typed identities transactionally. Resolve geometry, materials, joints, stores, sensor regions, controller inputs and animation targets. Reject dangling/foreign references, incompatible ports, duplicate ownership or conflicting property writers.
3. Build the transitive capability and effect closure: a heater may require heat, phase, topology and changed contact; an actuator may require electrical supply, work accounting, constraints and contact. Include declared possible transitions, not just initially active paths.
4. Partition data into existing specialized storage for the admitted capabilities. Prepare typed GPU buffers for immutable definitions/topology, committed numerical state, candidate state, bounded interaction/transfer work and numeric results. Prepare C# discrete candidate state separately. Use stable mappings from instance IDs to storage; packing order cannot alter physical ordering.
5. Construct a deterministic dispatch/dependency plan. A dispatch selects a reusable kernel by typed capability/model/geometry combination and an input range. Static specialisation may remove unused work; it must not generate part-specific equations or bypass the same shared law.
6. Freeze the applicable workload limits: body/shape counts, contacts/constraints, network nodes/edges, material cells or samples, fragments/particles, ray/event paths, iteration/dispatch bounds, numerical admission envelope and output/transport capacity. The current limited mechanical profile in the [GPU contract](gpu-f16-physics.md) is a development admission surface, not the final supported universe.
7. Validate the whole plan and reserve required state/publication resources before atomic installation. Unfrozen or unimplemented required capability, unsupported coupling or exhausted quota rejects the entire candidate with the typed reason and preserves the previous construction. Commit topology, physical seeds, controller registrations and animation bindings through the existing acknowledged lifecycle barrier.

The compiler decides structure and admission. It does not compute a substitute physical trajectory, constitutive result or numerical correction in C#. GPU shape/query admission is authoritative; CPU editor previews remain non-authoritative.

## Execute physical time with bounded shared WGSL kernels

The Simulation worker schedules the configured commits and physical substeps under the [shared master clock](shared-clock-cadence.md). Current defaults remain S120/A60/P60; alternate internal profiles require their existing admission/qualification. This design adds no player frequency controls or new timing-baseline prerequisite.

Each complete commit candidate starts from the last committed checkpoint and retains that checkpoint for rollback. Its physical substeps advance the preceding candidate endpoint in order; they never restart from the checkpoint or publish independently. C# admits ordered commands and discrete policy once at their existing declared tick/phase boundaries, not once again for every substep; WGSL evaluates physical conditions and candidate motion at the required phase. The engine runs the relevant shared domain kernels over compact capability batches. Conservative geometry queries provide candidates; actual shape/material laws decide contact, transmission, containment and exposure. Every consumer samples the same candidate physical time and geometry revision.

Dependencies that form an acyclic graph execute in declared order. Coupled feedback groups require an explicitly supported solve contract: participating unknowns, residual/error criteria, canonical update/reduction order and a bounded iteration/dispatch budget. The general plan does not assume one pass suffices for electrical → heat → material strength → contact → mechanical work. A domain owner must freeze a jointly solved or explicitly justified staged method and independent whole-step oracle before admitting that coupling. If unavailable, reject the combination; do not lag a physical feedback edge by a frame, iterate without bounds or hide a CPU solve.

All executable physical laws, numerical networks, geometry queries, continuous sensor/integral evaluation and physical solves run as WGSL f16. Use separate dispatches for global phases, exclusive writes, colouring or deterministic staged reductions as required by the current numerical contract. No cross-workgroup spin barrier, assumed f16 atomic addition or C# compensation lane is allowed. C# may inspect typed completion/failure flags and orchestrate the next bounded phase.

Cross-domain exchange has a common semantic record: source and sink store IDs, quantity/unit, proposed signed amount, corresponding work/impulse/enthalpy, material/species identity, physical interval and declaration/transaction identity. Numerical kernels produce proposals without debiting committed stores. Shared allocation considers all consumers of each finite source together, so two receivers cannot independently spend the same charge, heat or fluid. Accepted transfers update both sides and any declared environment flux; rejected predictions debit neither.

Mechanical/electrical/fluid/thermal/radiative conversions must declare losses and reactions. Examples include motor electrical debit and shaft work, pressure work and chamber internal energy, optical absorbed power and heat, and frictional mechanical loss with its declared heat/loss sink. A signal or animation event carries information, not free power. The exact conversion law remains the linked model owner's responsibility.

GPU completion, finite/range checks, conservation/error checks and capacity checks precede commit. Any failed candidate restores numerical buffers, discrete controller state, topology proposals, allocator/free state and pending reliable output to the same checkpoint. Publish no partial motion or success occurrence. Dynamic geometry, phase/fracture/spawn changes install as one bounded topology transaction with mass/inertia, material, queries and mappings updated together. Exact reset restores construction bits; reversing physics is never restoration.

## From observations to animation and rendering

Within a tick, prescribed discrete phases consume validated GPU candidate outputs and update candidate controller/goal/event state before the final joint commit; failure rolls those changes back with physics. Do not add a one-tick lag or move a required intra-tick transition after publication. External readers receive only the successful commit's coherent typed poses/trajectories, physical observables, topology changes and reliable occurrences. Numerical readings identify units, source, generation and revision; occurrences include stream/sequence and physical time. Controllers and goals follow their existing candidate-phase rules above; externally visible results are committed together. Continuous qualifying residence is a WGSL physical result; a C# latch can consume it. Accumulated dose across separated bursts is a different WGSL integral and must not be implemented by the residence timer.

The browser supplies value-only animation registrations and immutable target descriptors under the [binding contract](presentation-bindings.md). The Animation worker uses shared clip/easing/follow/impulse/oscillation and occurrence-lifecycle evaluators over active instances. Each registration defines its input channels, direction/range, clock projection, target property and lifecycle. A new effect requires a reusable evaluator extension if existing kinds cannot express it; putting a per-element callback inside AnimationHost is forbidden.

Autonomous UI tracks use continuous master-session time and can run without physics. World feedback uses the declared paused-world projection and identified committed observations/events. Event admission reserves the complete fan-out before acknowledgement. Present-once effects retain their bounded instance until an actual visible receipt or explicit removal/Reset; a timer expiry cannot invent visibility. Late/reordered/duplicate/stale events obey the existing reliable and generation rules. Disabled/reduced-motion effects cannot mutate physical results.

The browser samples one coherent display time, applies committed physical parents and cosmetic child outputs, and performs only declared final mappings/resource operations. There is one writer per target property. Physics never reads the drawn transform back. Descriptors may choose element-specific mesh, colour, sound or authored curve data; they cannot choose physical equations or private animation evolution. Physical motion, bounded readback, Animation evaluation and browser application have separately measured costs.

Run, Pause, Step, Resume, Completed, Reset, Load, disposal and fault recovery retain their existing atomic acknowledged semantics. Reset retires world physics/history/controller/animation generations, restores exact construction/baselines and releases leases; standalone UI follows its separate lifetime. Unsupported runtime save remains unsupported: the durable format is construction-only.

## Worked assemblies and discriminating controls

**Basketball and Receiver:** the ball declares sphere geometry/material, mass, gravity and drag; Receiver declares its physical box assembly and a typed sensor region/reference. The same shared contact kernels handle ball/workbench and ball/Receiver. WGSL finds continuous containment/relative-speed intervals and qualifying residence for the named body; exit, excessive speed or disabled sensing breaks residence. A reusable discrete latch emits one occurrence. That occurrence drives a shared halo definition, while physical poses stay committed authority. Preserve default margin 0.02, speed 1.5, dwell 0.35 and zero guide. Edge/fast-through, duplicate event, changed declaration instance and exact Reset distinguish the general path from an identity-based capture script. CAT-004-D owns the current admission/model freeze.

**Motor, conveyor and trapped cargo:** electrical power debit feeds a finite-work shaft actuator; the conveyor's surface-per-radian binding, contact/friction and load reaction consume the same committed shaft state. An obstruction cannot leave cargo moving because artwork spins. Decorative cues use shared followers and may be disabled without changing work or motion. The [transmission](rotary-transmission-parts.md) and [drive](bounded-acceleration-drives.md) models supply the physical law; disconnected/exhausted supply and blocked cargo are required controls.

**Fan or bellows driving a rotor:** source and receiver assemblies declare the same work-conjugate ports, finite reservoir/supply and transfer coefficients. Shared WGSL allocation divides one source force/power allowance across linear and rotary branches, including paired reactions and source debit. The fan's declared flow and bellows' slider-derived flow are coefficients/input bindings, not catalogue branches. Rotor pitch/resistance and passive damping use the [rotor](rotor-airflow-contract.md) and [transfer](linear-airflow-cutover.md) laws. Disconnected, blocked, unloaded emission, multiple receivers and exhausted supply distinguish supported transfer from hidden free thrust; unloaded gas emission still needs the [nozzle model](finite-gas-foundation.md).

**Heated water driving a piston:** electrical dissipation adds enthalpy to a finite vessel; the phase model converts conserved liquid to vapor, gas/nozzle transfers carry mass/enthalpy, and the generic pressure actuator exchanges work with a loaded body and exhaust. TH-17/19/35 differ in geometry/ports, not boiling or piston code. Empty vessel, inadequate latent energy, blocked exhaust and excessive load must fail differently. Exact phase/transport coupling remains S543/S550/S565 and S471/S419 design work; this route is not a newly approved boiling/turbine equation.

**Lens, combustible target and fusible support:** optical transport allocates absorbed power to the target's thermal store; shared reaction/material laws determine ignition or strength, then a topology transaction releases an actually loaded joint. Defocus, nonreactive material, missing oxidizer and subthreshold heat are controls. A TH-22 bimetal thermostat instead needs two-material differential strain and physical contact hysteresis; it cannot reuse a temperature switch as an equivalent mechanism. These missing constitutive decisions stay with S554/S555/S557/S558/S566 and the named source owners.

**Radiation instrument chain:** a moving capsule's committed trajectory feeds typed group/material transport. RAD-09 accumulates exposure across bursts and retains it on supply loss while its contact is disabled; RAD-08 measures rate. RAD-14 bends Alpha and BetaMinus according to their distinct signed models; Photon is not reflected by that operation. RAD-17 moderator transfers fast to slow neutron population with declared energy accounting; absorption is a separate sink. Scintillation and thermoelectric output consume deposited energy through shared conversion/thermal/electrical ports. S606/S607 and the exact RAD owners must freeze the declared grouped transport models, explicit fictional/model limits and independent oracles before implementation.

**Lured character and cut rope:** a controller queries occlusion/range and chooses a typed locomotion intent; physics resolves contact and supported actuation. It cannot write an authored destination pose. Scissors declare contact geometry, cutting work and material failure, updating a rope's topology only after the shared fracture criterion. EL-082/083/086/087 and EL-066/068 therefore need explicit LAW-CONTROLLER/S563 models, not cosmetic walking or counterpart-type checks.

An existing capability binding is intentionally conservative traceability and may omit a required submodel. The source's exact behavior wins: identify the missing reusable primitive and owner in that same design stage, then extend the composition. No admitted source may be called covered merely because a broad capability such as GeometryQuery, FiniteLedger or ProgrammableController appears in its row.

First freeze the Receiver's general contact/sensor/halo composition and reuse the acknowledged current clock evidence; do not restart deferred synchronization diagnostics. Then follow the ready TODO-linked slices. Broader family decisions occur when their real dependencies are needed. This architecture enables incremental construction of the complete general engine without requiring every future domain before the next playable outcome.

Acceptance of this document proves design coverage and ownership only. Runtime acceptance still requires actual shared WGSL execution, generic declaration substitution/parameter controls, unsupported-input rejection, independent numeric/conservation oracles, current UI behavior for each part/mode/fixture, exact lifecycle/save support, builds, bounded resources/performance and independent publication at their existing gates. P0-035 and the final product gate cannot close with unresolved required model or qualification gaps.
