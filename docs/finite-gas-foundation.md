## Bounded current sealed-store capability (12.1b)

The [Balloon native spec](../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-12-1b-balloon.md) owns current implementation and evidence. Build preparation preserves reference mass through reversible adiabatic equilibrium, explicitly accounting ambient boundary work and envelope potential. Standard/Vacuum are typed persisted construction boundaries under EL-125; Vacuum is an admissible boundary, not an empty gas store or a hidden substitute.

An open outlet applies q=conductance×max(Psource−Pboundary,0) with finite allocation. Isobaric loss retains temperature, reduces m/V/U together and accounts removed enthalpy, ambient compression work and released envelope potential. Canonical empty state is m=V=U=0. Isotropic outflow carries translational momentum and kinetic energy; centered gas inventory has no rotational inertia. One mass source owns dry carrier plus exact current stores. Actual sphere radius and committed art share gas volume plus declared dry core volume.

Accepted approaching-contact impulse for one unordered collider pair can latch an exact integrity observation and open its exact store once; speculative solver trials cannot debit gas or publish the occurrence. Discharge begins the following accepted substep. Rounded residuals clamp/continue rather than faulting a tick.

### Story 12.2 bounded airflow delivery

The [native Fan plan](../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-12-2-fan.md) specifies exact source/receiver identities, conical sample geometry, shared budgets, accepted-work debit and observations. Existing 120 Hz electrical storage charging is not exposed-only demand: the new generic continuous-work target must arbitrate with charging loads against the same source phase budget. Several airflow sources sharing a store never copy its balance. Each finite work/electrical/phase balance has one shared conservative f32 bulk/remainder pair; accepted per-commit joules, rather than subtraction of rounded cumulative observations, govern debit/credit. Charging, discrete releases, contact work and continuous work use that same settlement; Reset clears the remainder and rejected trials restore it with the ordinary snapshot. Due discrete chamber releases retain full-charge admission and execute first in admitted request order, debiting actual solved work; later phase credits cannot retroactively fire a refused request. Existing phase credits occur next, then continuous sources share the remaining store by requested joules u×impulse, with a fixed proportional reservation and no same-substep redistribution of unused grants. A moving nozzle receives the opposite force and complete moment, including the offset couple. Source work equals paired mechanical work plus nonnegative jet dissipation. Ideal shaft conversion pays that source work through the actual coordinate using the native plan's nonreversing coupled root/continuation and complete ordinary-substep replay; nonlinear extracted work is not a proportional reaction scale. The pre-integration midpoint work window owns the load debit, while later relaxation/restitution remains inside trial validation with its own contact/transmission work. No rejected trial retains balances, work or events. It has no extra conversion loss and emits nothing with a stopped/disconnected input; Motor's existing electrical drain remains deferred. Exact declared same-instance collider exclusions identify internal emitting geometry, including the physical hub; neither a frame-body nor an owner-wide exclusion is implicit. A non-exempt same-owner collider remains an occluder. Package identity never selects these laws.

This is planning, not runtime qualification. Full gas networks, Bellows unloaded emission and Windmill rotary receiver integration retain their named later owners. Existing harvest scenarios below retain their owners and browser deferral; they are not all Story 12.2 prerequisites.

### Laws at puzzle scale

- **Linear transfer.** One source allowance supplies every connected branch jointly; branches are allocated without mutating the candidate pose, paired nozzle/receiver reactions preserve linear and angular momentum, and the source reservoir debits exactly the work delivered plus paired loss. A disabled source plate, a blocked or missed path and an exhausted reservoir each transfer zero. Stationary receivers may accept impulse with zero receiver work. The balanced transfer abstraction has zero ambient exchange and no unloaded thrust; unloaded emission needs the nozzle record.
- **Rotary capture (Story12.3 native implementation/review complete; publication pending).** The rotor is a hinge-constrained rigid body with finite inertia, passive calm-air resistance b and typed mechanical output. Response g has units rad/s/N; cut-in is the magnitude of signed net aligned rest force in N. Each actual-pose sample contributes a capped nonnegative slip branch with relative speed v + p·alignment·ω. The same impulse supplies axial frame/nozzle reaction and work-conjugate hinge torque, with complete paired moments and per-source debit through the current continuous-work settlement. The compiler resolves exact frames/coordinates and scalars; simulation prepares feasible positive pitch and nonnegative resistance from current exposure/availability at each substep. For active nonzero rest force R0 at or above cut-in, requested Ω=clamp(g·R0,−Ωmax,+Ωmax) comes from the SAME frozen candidate exposure/velocity/supply state. Define H(p)=sign(Ω)·R(p,Ω), H(0)>0: use pmax if H stays positive through pmax, otherwise the FIRST boundary of that connected positive interval. Zero is the upper root bracket even on a plateau; retain b=p·R(p,Ω)/Ω≥0. Freeze poses/axes/sample velocities, target and allocation coefficients throughout the scalar search; changed outer trial allocation recalibrates instead of carrying old p/b. Fixed nonnegative coefficients give uncapped derivative −|Ω|·c·a²; clipping/capping adds flat segments, preserving scalar monotonicity even with moving offsets/opposing alignment, without claiming full coupled-solve monotonicity or convergence. With u=1,c=.75,g=4, first-boundary p=1/3,b=0 gives driving torque below3rad/s and zero at AND above3; strict opposing torque above equilibrium belongs to ordinary positive-damping cases, not this plateau. Below cut-in, active capture is zero and b=pmax/g is passive. Joint allocation/calibration and accepted work use the same candidate state; no copied budget or unused-grant redistribution. Source-free motion cannot gain energy. The solver never assigns/clamps shaft velocity or adds independently funded torque: external backdrive may exceed the requested unloaded cap. Front/rear drive, edge-on/opposing controls and partial occlusion follow the four declared weighted samples. Exact scope, preparation, controls and lifecycle are in the [native Windmill plan](../_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/spec-12-3-windmill.md), whose corrected artifact passed independent consistency review. Independent implementation review identified nine repairs; the approved pass-1 repair plan is implemented with final full7232/0/0, normal hooks134/0/0 and E9 PASS recorded in the native spec. All findings and final scope are independently approved; exact-SHA SnapshotApproval/publication remain pending, and browser/remote/release qualification is unverified. Earlier native verification remains historical candidate evidence. Body ownership is explicit current-candidate metadata, independent of constraint topology; exact source/capture frame, moving-coordinate and collider exclusions still require the declared owner.
- **Sealed gas.** Homogeneous single-species calorically perfect ideal gas. A reversible adiabatic volume change gives U₂ = U₁·(V₁/V₂)^(R/Cv) with unchanged mass; work on the gas is U₂ − U₁ and expansion returns negative work. Gas energy gain debits mechanical work and expansion credits the same work in the same substep. A charged store requires positive m/V/U within material limits at admission. Explicit Vacuum boundaries and canonical empty runtime stores are distinct valid states; malformed partial-zero, non-finite or out-of-limit authored states are refused, never replaced by a hidden ambient default.
- **Nozzle and finite discharge.** A converging nozzle declares NoFlow, Subsonic and Choked regimes with mass continuity, stagnation enthalpy, momentum and pressure thrust. Finite adiabatic discharge cools a rigid reservoir; final mass, internal energy and outlet enthalpy are accounted. A Bellows emits unloaded flow through its nozzle record and refills from its declared environment with 4 N·s/m refill damping; receiver impulse alone never substitutes for emission.
- **Envelope.** Region and transfer accelerations pass through the shared clamps (64 m/s² linear, 1024 rad/s² angular; directly authored acceleration fields A ≤ 16 m/s²; force-rated airflow transfers have no additional 16 m/s² cap). All affected impulses, paired moments/reactions and work scale together when global bounds limit transfer; never debit unapplied work. Rounding residual in work, inventory or pressure is clamp-or-continue under the [game-grade envelope](gpu-f32-physics.md#game-grade-envelope); it never creates energy, never faults a tick and never drops elapsed time. No free energy: a Windmill in still air never starts turning, a Fan with an empty reservoir pushes nothing, and simultaneous consumers cannot overdraw one store.
