# General-engine source composition map

This static design view accompanies [the execution design](../general-engine-design.md). It preserves **365 separate source identities**: 72 CAT and 216 EL + 37 TH + 22 RAD + 18 GAP. These are not 365 implemented or adopted new products. Potential, conditional, duplicate/refinement and umbrella dispositions remain those of the linked exact source; an umbrella never substitutes for its named children.

**Rows are declaration data.** Each row maps one element (CAT/EL/TH/RAD/GAP) to the generic capability families it instantiates plus its parameters, drawn from the [71-capability vocabulary](../coverage/engine-capabilities.json); the single WebAssembly SIMD f32 physics solver on a dedicated Web Worker consumes the physical families and the separate animation model consumes the animation families, under the [compilation model](../gpu-f32-physics.md#compilation-model). A row never names a solver, kernel branch or update loop of its own. The source owner's Design criterion freezes parameters, topology, supported modes, controller bindings, **animation bindings** (the element's declared curves/tracks/transitions and target properties, carried by the `AnimationEvaluation`/`AnimationLifecycle` families in the capability column and the element's binding shard) and the player-observable expected result before implementation. A capability label is not proof that the behavior is expressible or implemented; a row stays unqualified until its element plays in Chrome at its roadmap slice.

**Capability families by domain (all seven domains of the game):** gravity/mechanical contact — RigidBodyDynamics, ContactImpulse, SlidingFriction, JointConstraint, ElasticStorage, TensionTransmission, ShaftTorque, FiniteWorkActuation, EnvironmentState, AerodynamicDrag, Buoyancy, FieldForce, GranularTransport, StructuralFracture, DifficultyAssistance; electrical — ElectricalPower, SignalPropagation, ElectricalDissipation, ThermoelectricConversion; light/optics — OpticalTransport, OpticalAbsorption; heat — SensibleHeat, ThermalConduction, ThermalConvection, ThermalRadiation, ThermalExpansion, ThermalStress, HeatPumping, PhaseStorage, TemperatureSensing, TemperatureStrength, SolidMelting, LiquidFreezing, LiquidEvaporation, LiquidBoiling, VaporCondensation, SolidSublimation, VaporDeposition, ChemicalReaction, Ignition, Extinction, MaterialCoating; acoustics — AcousticPropagation; fluid/gas — FluidAdvection, PressureWork, GasState, CapillaryTransport, PhaseTopology; radiation — IonizingTransport, RadioactiveDecay. Shared stores, sensors and discrete state — FiniteLedger, GeometryQuery, TopologyTransaction, TimedCommand, ObjectiveEvaluation, ProgrammableController. Animation — AnimationEvaluation, AnimationLifecycle (separate worker, one-way from committed physics). The remaining families (compiler, persistence, transport, clocks, presentation, measurement) are host services. Each physical family is added to the solver by the slice that first needs it; domain-specialised kernels selected by declared data are valid, element-selected kernels are not.

To keep the table readable, common compiler/typed-contract/transaction, persistence, transport/clock, presentation/resource and measurement/admission services are omitted from the displayed composition. **Their exact per-source membership is retained in the linked existing binding shards**, not removed or assumed universal. Every row also inherits the general record, coupling, publication and lifecycle rules of the design. The domain routes name model prerequisites; existing source contracts with independently approved models are reused rather than reopened.

**Applicability is mode-specific:** domain routes below activate only when the exact admitted source mode/interaction needs that behavior. They are not new dependency edges or a demand to finish all conservative inventory memberships first. A dry Basketball drop with zero buoyancy reuses its current gravity/drag/contact model and does not wait for S416 liquids or every S470 gas law; its future fluid integrations remain required at their existing gates. Reuse an already frozen applicable model. Missing model checks for unrelated modes stay with those modes. Source-specific corrections in the decision column take precedence over broad inherited memberships, without silently rewriting the inventory or upgrading proof.

**Execution readiness is separate from membership:** use the [rolling playable roadmap](invest/vertical-delivery.md#rolling-playable-roadmap) (ENGINE-CORE-1/2, then one element per slice, ANIM-1, LEGACY-0, CAMPAIGN), current TODO and each source's actual admitted capability/model evidence to choose work. This map does not order all 365 identities before the next game interaction. Return to an already-supported source's remaining modes/fixtures and authored goal loops as well as introducing missing capabilities; a first-mode demonstration does not consume its full row. Future/conditional source dispositions and every exact child remain unchanged.

## Exact fixture and mode coverage

All **302 Fixture source IDs** are the entries with `Source.Origin=Fixture` in the indexed [fixture shards](../coverage/engine-capabilities.json), cross-checked against every fixture binding in [catalogue-elements](../coverage/catalogue-elements.json) and [source-inventory](../coverage/source-inventory.json). Their exact `level/instance` identity sets and the current 104 `Source + Dimension + Choice` mode tuples are retained in the scoped design review snapshot; no fixture is represented by a count alone. The mode records live in [catalogue-01](../coverage/engine/catalogue-01.json). All 72 Configuration=Fixed placeholders are inventory records, not a claim that no other mode exists.

A fixture composes its referenced catalogue declarations with its actual geometry, property values, difficulty, typed links and environment. It inherits that CAT's capability/model route, then adds every required interaction/transitive effect from its own exact source. Qualify it independently through actual UI. A nominal CAT capability list cannot suppress a mode, companion part, runtime transition or fixture-only requirement. Newly discovered source-required modes remain open under the same owner; do not renumber or merge records.

## Reading a decision route

S416 (liquid), S470 (gas), S484 (optical), S528 (acoustic), S543 (heat/phase), S605 (radiation), S257 (typed ports) and the LAW owners refer to the existing [bounded decisions](invest/decisions.md) and [requirements](requirements.md). They refine the source owner, not replace it. The main design gives the missing state/operation/coupling and discriminating controls for each route. All original linked source acceptance—including distinct variants and negative cases—remains binding.
