# Element-spec decisions, 22 November 2026

Binding on every element spec, slice contract and audit. Items marked **owner** were decided by the owner on 22 November 2026; items marked **coordinator** were decided under the owner's standing delegation (decide by game intent; scope cuts and waived gates go to the owner) and remain overridable by the owner. Cite an owner item as "owner decision N, 22 November 2026" and a coordinator item as "coordinator decision N, 22 November 2026". These replace any earlier "Needs the owner" entry on the same point.

The engine-conformance standard that every spec meets is in [engine-conformance.md](engine-conformance.md).

## Owner decisions

Earlier owner decisions these specs rely on (21 November 2026, from that day's question rounds): an acoustic screen (EL-050) blocks and attenuates only and does not reflect; the pool table's no-gravity variant B (EL-098) is decided at that slice's planning; a radiation source keeps fixed strength within a Run and is restored on Reset (RAD-01).

0. **Conformance standard (owner instruction).** Every element spec conforms to a generalised engine with no custom per-element logic, data-driven components, visuals separate from colliders separate from animations, and a proper high-performance physics engine. See [engine-conformance.md](engine-conformance.md).
1. **Connection strands: solid where thick.** Ropes, cables, hoses and ducts are solid: they get colliders. Thin wires stay non-solid drawings (as delivered), and the Balloon string keeps its earlier exception. A general, element-free capability for a colliding flexible strand belongs to `ENGINE-CONNECTION-MATERIALS`: the collider of a strand is a property of the declared connection material (declared diameter, segment rule), never per-element code, with cost bounded by compiled declarations. Until that story is delivered, each such spec is "blocked on ENGINE-CONNECTION-MATERIALS (solid strand)", and delivered parts that draw a thick strand without a collider (the delivered rope) carry a recorded visible-solid defect owned by that story. Do not write "non-solid tether" for any rope, cable, hose or duct.
2. **Delivered mechanical connections stay rigid.** No delivered connection becomes a slipping belt. Drive belt (EL-200) and drive chain (EL-201) are new declared connection materials that a player chooses; the existing rigid connection is unchanged and remains the default. Their tension default is chosen for the new parts only (160 N is the worked value); a visible belt or chain loop is thick and therefore solid under 1.
3. **Engine performance work is scheduled now.** Scene-proportional passes (sensors, airflow, membrane/compliant surface, Cannon queries, prismatic power bound, contact events) and island sleeping become general engine stories ahead of further element delivery. Specs name the owning story and never design around the gap with element logic.
3a. **Engine order.** Before any further element delivery, in this order: `DOC-ENGINE-STATUS`; `ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES` (persistent trees, one query interface for every phase); `ENGINE-CONTACT-OBSERVATIONS`; `ENGINE-SENSOR-VOLUMES` (shape-based, frame-relative, in-tick, several per element); `ENGINE-ISLANDS-SLEEP`; `ENGINE-ONE-WAY-INSTALL` (remove physics' wait for animation/renderer acknowledgement — physics commits alone); `ENGINE-SUPPLY-DEMAND` (sprint key `engine-supply-drive-demand`; the names `powered-part-demand-drain` and `engine-powered-part-demand-drain` are retired); `ENGINE-CAPABILITY-INSTANCES` (naming and fixed-slot refactors); then `ENGINE-TICK-ALLOCATION` (allocation-free tick) and `ENGINE-SOA-SIMD` (flat SoA tables, SIMD narrowphase/contacts), also before further elements. Before their first consumer: `ENGINE-JOINT-KINDS`, `ENGINE-MECHANICAL-LINKS`, `ENGINE-TENSILE-EXTENSIONS`, `ENGINE-SIGNAL-NODES`, `ENGINE-COMPLIANT-FRICTION`, `ENGINE-PARAMETER-BOUND-FRAMES`, `ENGINE-PROFILE-COLLIDER`, and the existing engine-first stories amended to take candidates from the spatial query interface. Use these names; do not coin another for the same capability. **Performance criterion:** each performance story states a measured before and after on a fixed reference scene and must not regress; the overall tick target is confirmed at the performance gate. Every element spec says "blocked on the engine programme (owner decision 3a)" in addition to any specific story.

24c. **Every part is used by the campaign (owner, 23 November 2026).** If no campaign lesson uses a placeable part or fixture, a lesson that uses it is added to the campaign, in the chapter that teaches its domain and after the parts it depends on.
24d. **Campaign shape (owner, 23 November 2026).** The campaign may exceed 150 levels. Levels that repeat a mechanic already introduced are removed: each mechanic is introduced once. Later levels build on earlier ones, combining and extending mechanics already taught so difficulty and complexity rise across the progression.
24e. **Chapter density (owner, 23 November 2026).** No level introduces more than two or three new parts. Where a chapter has too many first uses, first move introductions into earlier chapters wherever their prerequisites allow, then add levels for what remains. The campaign grows as needed.

24b. **Companions are not placeable (owner, 23 November 2026).** A companion package (owner decision 15: sealed can, toy fish, glow fish, toast slice, balloon cargo, fracture fragments) can be declared not placeable: it has no palette entry and appears only with its parent. The coverage follow-on story S7 adds that capability with the companion admission. One identity per placeable part.
24a. **Element order after the engines (owner, 23 November 2026):** once the 3a programme and the gas, liquid and thermal engines have landed, element slices are delivered in the order the campaign levels first teach them, whichever engine capability they need (not all gas/liquid/thermal consumers first, and not all capability-free parts first). Each element still waits for its own Depends-on stories.
24. **Approved defaults (owner, 23 November 2026):** construction parts show their limit arc and stress cue at all times; the material-density requirement row is reworded to a ratio to the game's liquid; "buoyancy" for the three ball identities means the declared ambient lift, so they close with one test each; the four thermal requirement rows with visual wording are reworded to the declared presentation bindings; ice meltwater that runs off a solid support counts as spilled.

## Coordinator decisions (standing delegation; overridable)

25. **From the propagation pass (23 November 2026).** Owner decision 6's declared spring rest position applies to every spring-returned blade, hatch or gate that must close when unpowered (including EL-184's blade and EL-109's hatch), not only the Powered gate and Beam shutter; specs re-size their rows to it. 26. **From the conventions re-audit (23 November 2026).** The finite store that absorbed light or radiation credits is owned by `ENGINE-THERMAL` (it is the same constant-or-credited heat/energy store row thermal declares), in all five conventions. The edge between a gas node and a liquid node is owned by `ENGINE-LIQUID` (it lands after `ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK`) and debits the gas store for the work it does. The gas step runs every substep (as the code does), before the liquid step. Heat moves with each liquid volume transfer, per substep, at the source store's heat per unit volume. A gas store may hold a declared mixture (air and water vapour) with partial pressures by Dalton's law, owned by `ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK`. Absorbed light or radiation is booked once: the absorbing collider's record is the only credit, and thermal reads its heat from that record (no second credit from a reading); a reading is flux through an aperture, not energy absorbed. Light and radiation share one near-field rule and one own-body rule (the optical forms). Every rate is written per tick duration `dt`, never as a fixed 120 Hz. Per-tick bounds come from compiled declarations with no typed refusal for exceeding a scene capacity (design rule D5). A diverging beam tests only receivers its cone query returns through `ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES`. The liquid ledger gains `Evaporated` and the thermal ledger gains sink, source and work terms limited so boiling is counted once.
27. **From the second audit of formerly Not-ready specs (23 November 2026).** A supplied drive whose request in a tick exceeds its source's per-tick allowance (`power_limit × dt`; 1 J per tick for the default Battery at 120 Hz) is limited to that allowance in that tick: its torque or force is scaled so the work it does that tick equals the energy granted (`ENGINE-SUPPLY-DEMAND`); specs whose rows assume more are re-derived. A 45° bend is a placeable palette part, so it is its own ledger identity (proposed in Batch I's summary), not a companion package (owner decision 15 covers parts that are not placed by themselves). Each package has one declaration text: where a catalogue (CAT) spec and a named-identity spec describe the same package, the CAT spec is the single declaration and the named spec points to it.

Buoyancy in liquid stays the liquid conventions' displaced-volume law (EL-001 L12).

The seven capabilities the merge found without an owner take the recommended defaults, now written into their owning stories and listed in the [engine stories index](engine-stories-index.md), "Capabilities formerly without an owner", except buoyancy in air, which coordinator decision 28 settles.
