## 2. Findings on the liquid conventions (EL-001), most severe first

**C-1. Level and tilt retention use two different geometries for the same store (physical incoherence).** `EL-001-finite-reservoir.md:29` (L4) sets depth to V/A at any pose. `:36` (L11) sets the retained capacity from the tilted wedge. For the gutter at 5° these disagree by a factor of ten. `EL-007-open-gutter.md:52` gives contents 0.062 m³ at 0.051 m depth; the wedge L11 implies holds 0.40 × 0.051² / (2 × 0.0875) = 0.0060 m³. `EL-007:53` says the gutter is at its limit (0.091 m³) with 0.076 m of depth; L11's own limit is the surface at the 0.20 m rim, where the outflow is 0.21261 × √0.20 = 0.095 m³/s, not 0.059. So row G4 (`:99`, a bare tank mouth at 0.0835 m³/s overtops) is false under L11 and true under L4. The same split reaches every head read on a tilted store: the bucket, the leak (`EL-015-leaky-bucket.md:59` admits it), probes and joined mouths.
Replacement for L4's level sentence: "Depth and surface come from volume and committed tilt by one relation. For an interior of width w along the tilt, breadth b = A/w and height H, tilted by θ: depth normal to the floor at the low edge is d = √(2·V·tan θ / b) while d ≤ w·tan θ, otherwise d = V/A + (w·tan θ)/2. Surface elevation = elevation of the lowest floor datum + d·cos θ. At θ = 0 this is d = V/A." Replacement for L11: "The retained capacity C(θ) is the volume at d = H in L4." (This reproduces both of L11's present formulas.) Then EL-007 `:52`: contents 0.0060 m³; `:53`: carrying limit 0.095 m³/s for any slope that keeps the far end dry; G1 boundary (`:96`): "contents settle near 0.006 m³ within 0.004 m³"; G4: feed from two bare tank mouths (0.167 m³/s) or delete the row and keep overtopping in SG4.

**C-2. Overflow cascades make the step unbounded, against L18.** `EL-001:34` (L9) emits overflow "in the same step" as a stream, and `:33` (L8) credits a stream "in that same step" to what it meets, which may itself be full (EL-004 row B4, basin over basin). A chain of n full vessels needs n dependent resolutions in one substep, and a pumped loop has no end. `:43` (L18) claims work "bounded by the compiled counts". Replacement for L9's second sentence: "Each substep is one pass: aperture flows from committed heads, then stream credits. Volume credited above C(θ) is held as that store's pending overflow (part of its ledger volume, never read as head) and leaves from the overflow point in the next substep. A cascade therefore advances one vessel per substep (1/480 s)."

**C-3. Conservation at a fitting is not guaranteed.** `EL-001:32` (L7) requires flows at every fitting to sum to zero; `EL-011-water-t-junction.md:71` and `R-f:108` allow "a declared fixed number of sweeps". Two fittings separated by a seat (tee, tap, tee) are coupled unknowns; a fixed sweep count leaves a residual, and the residual is created or destroyed liquid. L10 and the proposed acceptance ("every committed tick satisfies…") then fail. Add to L7: "After the declared sweeps, outflows of each fitting are scaled to its inflows (one recovery inside the solve), so a fitting never stores or creates volume; the unconverged remainder shows only as a flow error within the 10% tolerance."

**C-4. "First solid met spills the stream" is the wrong general rule; three parts are designed around it.** `EL-001:33`. Consequences in the specs: a float in a basin under a tap sends the stream to `Spilled` although it lands in the basin; `EL-017-mechanical-float-valve.md:25` moves the arm sideways so "the stream does not meet the float"; `EL-014-water-carrying-bucket.md:47` deletes the bail for the same reason; `R-f:116` proposes a per-material flag so ropes do not stop streams. The flag is a second rule for one behaviour, selected by kind (forward-only gate), and the art changes design around an engine rule (addendum point 6). One geometric rule covers all four cases. Replacement in L8: "A stream is a fixed, declared number of sample paths across its contracted section (radius √Cd × the aperture radius), each carrying an equal share. A sample that meets a collider inside a store's declared interior is credited to that store; a sample that meets any other collider is spilled. No collider is exempt by kind." A rope or bail then costs at most its shadow, a float in a basin costs nothing, and `ENGINE-CONNECTION-MATERIALS` needs no liquid clause. Delete `R-f:116`. This needs a declared interior (C-5).

**C-5. A store has no declared interior.** L4 declares capacity, area, datums and an aperture. L12 (`:37`), the depth probe (`EL-018-electronic-level-switch.md:26`), Batch O's attenuation by "a liquid store's committed fill" (`readiness/batch-o.md:53`) and C-4 all need the region the liquid occupies. Add to L4: "The interior is the open aperture's region extruded down to the floor datum plane; immersion, probes and other capabilities read liquid only inside it." Also L9 (`:34`) overflows on V above C only, so a ball dropped into a full basin lifts the surface above the rim (L4 adds V_displaced) and spills nothing. Replace "Volume that would take a store above C" with "Volume that would take V + V_displaced above C(θ)".

**C-6. Mouth ids and snap constants are built on what `ENGINE-CAPABILITY-INSTANCES` deletes; the third mouth has two owners.** `EL-001:30` adds a `Branch` member to `TubeEnd`; `:31` adopts the 0.45 m and 20° constants of `engine/TubeMouthSnap.cs:24-25`. `GAP` finding E5 (owner order 3a, story 8) replaces `TubeEnd` and those constants with "a declared list of snap sockets, each with its own capture distance and angle". `R-f:99` and `EL-011:58,120`, `EL-012:54`, `EL-020:61,114` give more-than-two and single mouths to `ENGINE-LIQUID`. Two owners for one capability. Also verified: `tools/BundleElements/SceneGeometryExtractor.cs:77-79` throws on any locator other than `Loc_Port_mouth_start` and `_end`, so `Loc_Port_mouth_branch` and `Loc_Outlet` are refused today; and Batch N asks for `StartB`, `EndB` (`readiness/batch-n.md:127`). Replacement in L5: "Mouths are declared snap sockets with package-local ids (`ENGINE-CAPABILITY-INSTANCES`); a part declares one or more. Each socket declares its capture distance and angle; the water kit declares 0.20 m and 20°." (0.45 m was sized for a 0.65 m bore; the tap is 0.30 m long.) W3 owner becomes `ENGINE-CAPABILITY-INSTANCES` for socket count and ids, `ENGINE-LIQUID` for compiling joins to edges.

**C-7. Gas and liquid claim one fluid port contract and use two.** Batch H joins `PortDescriptor` ports of `SimulationDomain.Fluid` by Output-to-Input connection links (`EL-039-air-reservoir.md`, F3). Batch F joins `TubeMouthDescriptor` mouths with a `FluidPortRole` by adjacency. Neither uses `FluidPortDescriptor` as it is (string `PortId`, `MaxFlowRate`, `OperatingPressure`; `CuriousContraptions.Abstractions/FluidContractValues.cs:12`), and direction is expressed twice (`PortDirection` and `FluidPortRole`). As drafted this is a join rule chosen by medium, which the forward-only gate forbids, and it will be met in practice: EL-052, EL-229, TH-17 and TH-18 put gas and liquid on one part. It is acceptable only as one contract with two socket kinds open to either medium. Proposed sentence for both stories: "A fluid port is one record: a node, a role, an aperture area and a socket. A socket is either a scene mouth joined by adjacency or a link terminal joined by a connection of a fluid connection material. Both compile to the same network edge; no join rule depends on the medium." One owner: `ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK` (cluster 3, before liquid) reshapes or deletes `IFluidElement`; `ENGINE-LIQUID` consumes it. `R-f:138` ("whichever lands first") is not an owner. D3's "no second join mechanism" needs the same amendment.

**C-8. Density 16 kg/m³ against 400 kPa air.** D2 (`docs/planning/invest/delivery-batches.md:163`) is a delegated decision, not an owner one, and its reason holds: Basketball 1 kg at 0.34 m is 6.1 kg/m³ and floats, Bowling ball 4 kg is 43.5 kg/m³ and sinks (`elements/Ball/BallElementDeclaration.cs:51`, `elements/BowlingBall/BowlingBallElementDeclaration.cs:54`). But L1 puts liquid node pressure on the gas scale, and 1 m of this liquid is 157 Pa. Batch H's default tank (400 kPa) over liquid is 1,900 m of head and a 194 m/s jet, three times the 64 m/s clamp. No convention says how a gas node meets a liquid node. Needed in L13 or the shared contract before EL-052, EL-229 or TH-17 is planned; see owner item 2.

**C-9. Stability argument is not in any spec.** `R-f:153` says "checked by hand calculation only" and records none. Mine, for the record: for two stores joined by one aperture, Δ changes per substep by c·√Δ with c = K·(1/A₁ + 1/A₂)/480. A step overshoots only when Δ < c², and then by at most c²/4. Worst cases: channel bore between two 1 m gutters, c² = 4.9 × 10⁻⁶ m; pipe bore between two 0.25 m² stores, c² = 1.9 × 10⁻⁶ m. Both are four orders below the 0.01 m tolerance, so the pairwise law is stable with or without L7's clamps. It is not established for: a store in the wedge regime of C-1 (effective area falls to zero with volume, so c is unbounded and only the "never more than it holds" clamp protects it; an inflow clamp is needed too); coupled fittings (C-3); a reported flow at equilibrium, which flips sign each substep at about K·c/2 (6 × 10⁻⁵ m³/s). L17 (`:42`) has no absolute flow allowance, so "no aperture reports flow" at a dynamically reached balance is an exact-zero assertion on a simulated float. Add to L17: "A flow below 0.0005 m³/s counts as none." Add C-9's bound to L7 so `ENGINE-LIQUID` planning has something to confirm.

**G-1. Every spec omits the engine programme (owner decision 3a) and the stories the liquid step depends on.** No spec or `R-f` row contains "engine programme", `ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES`, `ENGINE-ISLANDS-SLEEP` or `ENGINE-SIGNAL-NODES`; `R-f:118` says `GAP` was not read. L18 (`:43`) relies on "one broadphase query per store interior" and a path query per free end; `GAP` section 1 shows only contact pairs use the tree today. Fix in each spec's Delivery prerequisites and the `R-f` table: "blocked on the engine programme (owner decision 3a), then `ENGINE-LIQUID`". In L18 add: "stream and immersion candidates come from `ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES`; a change of contents or level wakes the islands it loads (`ENGINE-ISLANDS-SLEEP`); the liquid step is not repeated by a solver trial replay." The last matters: the motor and airflow budgets re-run whole substeps (`GAP` section 1, "Drive and continuous-work budgets"), and a ledger advanced twice breaks L10.

## 3. Findings on parts

**H-1. EL-017 cannot work as dimensioned.** `EL-017-mechanical-float-valve.md:97` puts the hinge 0.30 m above a Catch basin floor. The float (radius 0.20, 0.70 m out) then rests on the floor at −8°, so "hangs at −30°" (V5, `:105`) and the block "above the arm at its −30° position" (V2, `:102`) cannot occur. The tube axis is at hinge height, 0.10 m below the 0.40 m rim, so the supply pipe passes through the basin wall. Raising the tube clear of the rim (hinge at 0.54 m or more) holds the level at 0.50 m, above the rim. V3 (`:103`) expects 0.24 m between −10° and +10°; in this rig the −10° float sits on the floor. V4 (`:104`) drains "through a store edit", which is not a defined interface. One consistent set (author to confirm): hinge limits −45° to +5°; `shut_off_angle` default −20°, range −25° to −5°; opening band 15°; rig hinge 0.60 m above the basin floor. Held depth is then 0.60 − 0.70·sin 20° − 0.038 = 0.32 m, under the rim, with the tube 0.06 m clear of it. V4 becomes: the vessel is a Water tank whose mouth feeds a Tap at `opening` 0.125; the level settles in a band below the shut-off and the supply tank keeps falling.

**H-2a. EL-021 blade opens when inverted.** `EL-021-sluice-gate.md:25` declares 14 N/m with rest at 0 and says the blade "closes in every mounting orientation"; row SG5 (`:110`) asserts it. Inverted, gravity pulls a 1.0 kg blade open with 9.81 N; the spring gives 3.5 N at full travel. Equilibrium would be 0.70 m, beyond the 0.25 m travel: the blade rests fully open. (The delivered Powered gate has the same values, `elements/PoweredGate/PoweredGateElementDeclaration.cs:76-84`; owner decision 6 must therefore mean a preloaded rest position.) Replacement keeping blade and stiffness: "rest position −0.75 m (preload 10.5 N at closed)". Then `:46` reads: "Lifting needs 20.3 N at closed and 23.8 N at full lift. A 2.5 kg Weight (24.5 N) lifts it fully; 2.25 kg (22.1 N) holds it at 0.126 m; 2.0 kg (19.6 N) cannot lift it." SG1 uses 2.5 kg; SG3 uses 2.0 and 2.25 kg; SG4 uses a 2.14 kg Weight (0.05 m) in place of the undefined "pusher or a stop".

**H-2b. EL-021 and EL-007: a level gutter drains from its far end.** `EL-021:102` uses two level gutters. Both ends of a level gutter are wet (`EL-007:55`), and nothing in Batch F closes a channel mouth (the cap is pipe bore). So SG2 (`:107`, "the upstream gutter holds its 0.24 m³") and `:56` fail: the upstream gutter empties through its unjoined `Start`. Fix: slope the upstream gutter 5° toward the gate. Under C-1 it then retains 0.091 m³, fills in 0.091 / 0.0341 = 2.7 s, and SG2 reads "holds 0.091 m³ … full by 2.7 s within 10%".

**H-3. EL-016 FL3 does not prove "overloaded float sinks".** `EL-016-float.md:92` places a Bowling ball on a floating sphere. A sphere on a sphere is unstable; the ball rolls off and sinks alone, the float stays up, and "both rest on the basin floor" is false. The control (a Tennis ball, which floats by itself) passes whatever happens. Replacement: "Given a float in a Water tank holding 1.0 m³ inside a 0.60 m square well of four Wall blocks standing above the surface, and a Bowling ball lowered onto it, when Run advances 10 s, then both rest on the tank floor (combined 4.25 kg against 2.5 kg of lift). Control: a Tennis ball in the same well; the float's lowest point stays above the floor." Also `:47`: with c = 1.0 s⁻¹ the float bobs for about 25 s. Stable rest is an acceptance target, and the float valve's seat follows that arm. Under D1 this is a starting value; say so, and let `ENGINE-LIQUID` set c for a damping ratio near 0.5.

**H-4. EL-022 "closed when ungranted" removes the check valve's decision.** `EL-022-water-pump.md:64` closes an unpowered pump both ways. `sequence-task-389` (`docs/planning/requirements.md:3005`) requires the check valve to create the decision "prevent backflow". With the pump sealed there is none; and row M7 (`:122`) delivers through a free end above the rim, so nothing can return with or without the valve. The row cannot fail. Recommended: "an ungranted work target adds no head and passes flow as an open edge", so lifted liquid runs back through a stopped pump unless a check valve holds it; "unpowered pump cannot sustain uphill flow" still holds. M7 then joins `End` by pipe to the Header tank's mouth, cuts supply by a Battery `capacity` of 60 J (2 s at 30 W), and has the control "without the valve the Header tank drains back". M2's boundary (`:117`) changes to "passes downhill only". If the closed edge is kept, delete M7 and say EL-019 is proven by CV1 to CV3 alone.

**H-5. EL-018 names the wrong owner.** `EL-001:108`, `EL-018:72,76,138` and `R-f:30,112` give the threshold node to `ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK`. Owner decision 3a names `ENGINE-SIGNAL-NODES` for "threshold over a committed scalar" and forbids another name. Replace throughout.

**M-1. EL-022 has two input rules.** `:37`: Electrical debits what was spent (M5, a dry intake draws nothing); Shaft absorbs min(30/|ω|, 4)·|ω| whatever flows. Replacement: "Shaft: resisting torque = (hydraulic power ÷ efficiency) ÷ |ω|, never above min(30 W / |ω|, 4 N·m)". Same sentence in `R-f:99`.

**M-2. Kit parts do not sit on the bench with a bench tank.** Tank mouths are 0.15 m above the base (`EL-001:54`). The cap (radius 0.17, `EL-012-water-pipe-cap.md:24`) and the check valve bulge (0.18, `EL-019-water-check-valve.md:24`) go 0.02 and 0.03 m into the bench. The pump's `Start` is 0.10 m above its base (`EL-022:26`), so "a full Water tank on the bench joined to `Start`" (`:57`) puts the housing 0.05 m under the bench. Add to EL-008's kit values: "a pipe-bore mouth axis is 0.15 m above its part's base and no kit part reaches more than 0.15 m below its axis". Cap and bulge radius 0.15; pump mouths at y = −0.05 and +0.15.

**M-3. EL-002 H1 height.** `EL-002-header-tank.md:56,101`: a free end at 2.5 m from a mouth at 2.15 m. A 90° elbow alone ends at 2.55 m; elbow plus the shortest pipe at 3.05 m. Replace 2.5 with 2.55: H1 delivers 0.60 × 0.25 = 0.150 m³ in 2 × 0.25 × √0.60 / 0.05904 = 6.6 s (stall guard 20 s); H2 boundary at `stand_height` 1.5 delivers 0.025 m³. **M-4. EL-013 acceptance geometry.** `EL-013-water-nozzle.md:45,50`: exit at 0.14 m aimed 45° up. A nozzle joins only in line with its pipe (20°), so it needs an elbow; its exit is then at least 0.47 m above the bench. **M-5. Undefined fixture edits.** EL-004 has no parameter (`EL-004-catch-basin.md:28`), yet `EL-016:90` uses "a basin holding 0.4 m³", `EL-018:112` basins "authored at depths", `:113` "a ball dropped in and removed by the fixture". **M-6. EL-011 J3** (`:96`) compares accumulated gains but gives no duration; the higher outlet runs dry once the tank falls 0.05 m, so the ratio is not 3.2 for long. Replace with "the committed outlet flows at the first sampled tick are in the ratio 3.2 within 15%".

**M-7.
