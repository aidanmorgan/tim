## Blocking findings

### F1. M3 cannot reach `Query`: the compiled scene refuses a re-targeted track (wrong specified behaviour; inside the intent contract)

- **Where:** contract `:47` (M3 matrix row, intent), `:33` (Always), `:94` (probe), `:101`, `:142` (note 7), `:169` (table row).
- **Defect:** M3 takes the one Pulley's compiled `CommittedWheelAngle` track, changes only `Driver` and `Mapping` (plus `Socket` for the socket driver) and rebuilds `WorkshopCompiledScene`. The scene validates each track's driver-specific binding fields before the presentation exists (`engine/gpu/WorkshopCompiledScene.cs:223-227`):
  - `InvalidMechanicalObservationBinding` requires `(Driver == CommittedWheelAngle) == (Guide != 0)` (`:298-301`). The Pulley track's `Guide` is set, so every other driver is refused "Compiled presentation track is invalid." (an `ArgumentException`).
  - Independently, each other driver requires its own source field to name a real object of the owner, absent from a Pulley-only scene: `Activation` for the seven signal drivers (`InvalidSignalBinding` `:312-319`, `PresentationDriverRules.SignalSource` `ElementPresentation.cs:137-141`), `Source` for Supply/Charge (`:233-241`), a `LogicGate` `SupplyNode` for the socket driver (`:242-245`), `Airflow` (`:250-252`), `GasShape` (`:256-258`), `Store` for StoredFraction (`:268-271`), a revolute or supplied `Actuator` (`:277-292`), `Retention` and `Transmission` (`:302-307`); and `CommittedOscillatorPulse` admits only `LinearDecay` with a pulse overlap (`ElementPresentation.cs:285-288`).
  - The probe's evidence for "re-targeting a real compiled track" (`:94`, the test at `ComposableAnimationMappingTests.cs:392-402`) changes only `Curve`, `Response`, `Mapping`, `From` and `To`; it never changes a driver. That capability is not exercised.
- **Consequence:** If the scene rebuild is inside the asserted call, the scene's own `ArgumentException` satisfies "an `ArgumentException` whose message is not 'Unsupported committed presentation driver.'" for 18 drivers without `Query` ever running, so a missing table row passes: the test is vacuous for exactly the defect it exists to catch. If the rebuild is outside, 18 cases fail on the baseline and the contract's own clause (`:169`) sends the slice straight back to planning. Either way the S6 closing condition (owner decision 24h: a test that every enum value has a route) is not met for this chain.
- **Fix, `:47` (intent):** `| M3 | Each `PresentationDriver` value except `Event` that `PresentationDriverRules.SupportsMapping(value, PresentationValueMapping.Identity)` admits, taken from the enum; for each, a delivered part whose declared presentation binds that driver, compiled into a real scene and given its committed read | The presentation constructs and presents a sample for that part's binding of that driver | A value that no delivered part declares fails the case; a missing route surfaces "Unsupported committed presentation driver." and fails |`
- **Fix, `:33`:** "The route test runs a real compiled scene of a delivered part that declares each driver and the real presentation through their public constructors, takes its cases from the enum and the public `PresentationDriverRules`, finds the declaring part from the registered part definitions' presentation bindings (`PartDefinition.Presentation.Bindings`), and asserts behaviour, not the table's layout."
- **Fix, `:94`:** replace the bullet with "**Present:** compiling a scene of delivered parts and constructing the presentation from its committed read -- `ComposableAnimationMappingTests.WorkshopDeclaredPresentation_OneCurveWithDifferentSources_ExponentialFollow` (`:379-404`). Re-targeting a compiled track's driver is not a capability: the compiled scene refuses a track whose driver-specific source fields do not match its driver (`engine/gpu/WorkshopCompiledScene.cs:223-307`)."
- **Fix, `:169` (M3 row, first two cells):** "`ComposableAnimationMappingTests.EveryContinuousCommittedDriverHasACommittedQueryRoute` (new theory). Cases: `Enum.GetValues<PresentationDriver>()` minus `Event`, filtered by `PresentationDriverRules.SupportsMapping(value, PresentationValueMapping.Identity)` (19 at the baseline). Per case: the first registered part definition (`PartRegistry.Definitions`, ordered by `PartId`) with a presentation binding of that driver (at the baseline one of Cannon, Signal lamp or State latch, Counter, System clock, Delay box, Hold timer, an electrical gate, Battery, Clutch or Windmill, Motor, Pulley, Fan, Balloon, Trampoline, Wound spring); `Assert.NotNull` on it, so a driver no part declares fails; one instance placed with `WorkshopInput.Generic`, compiled with `CompiledSceneFixture.Compile`, one read from `WorkshopIntegrationHarness.Observe`; `new WorkshopDeclaredPresentation(...)` constructs without throwing (construction stages the read through `Query`, `WorkshopDeclaredPresentation.cs:154`, `:282-298`) and `Advance` returns a sample for that part's binding of that driver. If a declaring part cannot compile alone, its case uses the scene of that driver's cited M1 test; no case is dropped and no track field is edited. | Control by reading: a driver without a row throws 'Unsupported committed presentation driver.' at construction and fails its case. Boundary: continuous routes only; the six `StateValues` drivers and `Event` are S6c's (`delivery-batches.md` S6 Slices table)."
- **Fix, `:101` (Proof decision):** replace "The route theory re-targets a real compiled track" with "The route theory compiles a delivered part that declares each driver".
- **Fix, `:142` (note 7):** replace "Uncertain: whether the compiled scene admits every re-targeted track (M3 rule below)." with "Uncertain: whether every declaring part compiles alone (M3 falls back to the driver's cited M1 scene)."
- AC4's delta stays +19 (19 theory cases); the registration check stays not required (existing test file).
