# Planning audit: `spec-s6g-mechanical-kind-tables.md` (S6g, new), first audit (series 6)

Audited 24 November 2026 in the `spec-drafts` checkout at main `0959e883` (the contract was drafted on `25df3cd5`; the source files it cites are unchanged between the two), planning phase, read-only. Nothing built or run. Contract: 2,540 words (budget 6,000). Binding decisions read: `owner-decisions-2026-11-22.md` (22, 24h, 3a, 38, 42). Scans: ast-grep 0.51.3 with the S6b inventory's definition of a chain (a member naming two or more distinct members of one enum in a switch arm, `==`, `!=`, `is` or `or` position), member by member, over the S6b root list; `elements/`, tests, tools, generated code and `obj/` excluded. Scan scripts are in the coordinator's scratchpad (`scratchpad/s6g/`), not in the checkout.

## Verdict: Fail

The design direction is right and the slice is the correct size and tier (engine tier, one structural slice). But the intent contract (read-only after planning) contains a false premise in row G1 and a kept-comparison list that is wrong by the inventory's own rule, so the closing run G6 would print chains the contract neither owns nor keeps. The hot-path scene does not exercise two of the four hot paths it names, no row shape is given, G4 and G5 cannot be executed or counted as written, and the "closes S6" claim holds only by the letter of the exclusion list. Findings B1 to B8 block; each has the exact replacement text.

## Blocking findings

### B1. G1 rests on a false claim and a scene that cannot reach five of the coordinate sites (intent contract; wrong specified behaviour)

Contract `:43` (G1) and `:72` (probe). G1 says "no delivered transmission binds a prismatic coordinate" and picks `LinearPusher` as the prismatic scene. Both are wrong:
- `elements/WoundSpring/WoundSpringElementDeclaration.cs:53-54` declares a transmission whose driving coordinate is `(Revolute, 0)` and whose driven coordinate is `(Prismatic, 0)` (`NonPositiveVelocity`), with a prismatic retention at `:56`. `WoundSpringIntegrationTests`, `WoundSpringLifecycleTests` and `WoundSpringContractTests` exist (`CuriousContraptions.tests/`).
- `LinearPusher` binds its prismatic coordinate only as a supply drive target (`LinearPusherElementDeclaration.cs:54`) and a presentation actuator (`:75`). That reaches `WorkshopSupplyCompiler.cs:94`, `WorkshopSceneCompiler.cs:19` and `BundleValidator.cs:332`. It never reaches the transmission sites: `WorkshopPhysicsCompiler.cs:341` `ResolveCoordinate`, `WorkshopWireFlatBuffers.Compiled.cs:65-77` (`CoordinateIdentity`, `DecodeCoordinate`, carried only in the transmission record), `PhysicsGpuAbi.Constraints.cs:622` `ReadCoordinate`, `WorkshopPhysicsEngine.cs:738` `BindCoordinate` and `MechanicalTransmissionValidation.cs:48` `ValidCoordinate`. A prismatic row missing at any of those five would pass G1 as written.

Fix, replace G1's Input and Expected cells with: "For each `CoordinateKind`, a real scene through the production compile, admit, wire round trip and engine read: `WoundSpring` (driving `Revolute`, driven `Prismatic`, `WoundSpringElementDeclaration.cs:53`) reaches `ResolveCoordinate`, the compiled-scene codec, `ReadCoordinate`, `BindCoordinate` and `ValidCoordinate` for both kinds; `Clutch` (`ClutchElementDeclaration.cs:148`, revolute to revolute) is the revolute-only control; `LinearPusher` reaches `WorkshopSupplyCompiler.cs:94`, `WorkshopSceneCompiler.ResolveActuator` and `PresentationBindingActuator` for prismatic and `Motor` for revolute. Expected: round-trips and drives its binding as today. Control: a coordinate naming a missing joint is refused at `ResolveCoordinate` ('Unsupported constraint coordinate.' for an undefined kind, the existing bundle error for a missing index)." Delete the clause "no delivered transmission binds a prismatic coordinate" from G1 and the Code Map.

### B2. The "kept single-member comparison" list is wrong by S6b's own rule; G6 would fail (closure; wrong specified behaviour)

Contract `:60`, `:48` (G6), AC4. S6b's rule (spec-s6b `:57-67`) makes a chain any member naming two or more distinct members in comparison position. Two members the contract keeps are chains:
- `engine/gpu/PhysicsGpuAbi.Airflow.cs:90` `WriteAirflowInput`: `input.Kind == Store` (`:93`), `== Coordinate` (`:95`), `== Electrical` (`:98`). Three distinct members; a wide member. Contract lists only `:93` as kept.
- `CuriousContraptions.Simulation/WorkshopPhysicsEngine.ContinuousPrepare.cs:47` `PrepareContinuousSource`: `== Coordinate` (`:54`), `== Store` (`:66`, `:67`). Two distinct members; per tick, per source. Contract lists only `:54` as kept.
Both are the same per-kind routing (which slot column a source reads, which balance gates availability), i.e. a row column each, and `ENGINE-SUPPLY-DEMAND` will add a branch to both.

Fix: move both into owned scope under "Continuous-work inputs" with the row columns "ABI slot written" (`WriteAirflowInput`) and "slot offset and availability balance" (`PrepareContinuousSource`). Replace the "kept" list for this enum with exactly the members that name a single member, recorded with file:line in the run log: `ContinuousPrepare.cs:100` `JoinPhaseStorage` (`== Store`), `ContinuousSolve.cs:107` `ContinuousLoadDemand`, `ContinuousSolve.cs:136` `ContinuousPaymentsFit(kind)` (`:144-145`), `WorkshopPhysicsEngine.Supply.cs:60` and `:103`, `PhysicsGpuAbi.Airflow.cs:140` `ReadAirflowStoreDebits`, `AirflowSceneValidation.cs:95`, `SupplyRoute.cs:431`, `PhysicsGpuAbi.Electrical.cs:181`, `WorkshopCompiledScene.cs:182`. (`ContinuousSolve.cs:107` stays a hot-path comparison, unchanged.)

### B4. The hot-path reference scene does not run two of the four hot paths it claims (Boundaries `:31`, AC5 `:100`; owner decision 42)

### B5. No row shape is decided; the descriptor-keyed sites cannot be keyed by an enum as written (Ready for Development: actionable, complete)

Task 2 (`:90`) is "their tables -- G1 to G4". Missing, and needed before an implementer can start:

### B8. The S6 closure claim is true by the letter of the exclusion list only (closure; owner decision 24h)

Contract `:24`, G6, AC4; delivery-batches S6 "Closure". The closing run passes if every printed enum is in a list. By S6b's definition that holds (this audit found no enum with a comparison-position chain outside both lists; its scan over all 210 names printed none). But the exclusion text states that no excluded enum "has a switch, a ladder or a wide member" (spec-s6b `:91`), and that is false: this audit's scan finds 92 excluded enums with at least one member naming two or more of their values and 51 with three or more (for example `ActivationTimerPhase` `ElementPresentation.cs:63`, `AnimationCadence` `CadenceValues.cs:60`, `SimulationCadence` `WorkshopGpuProfile.cs:9`, `BoxFace` `MechanicalContracts.cs:97`). Most are lifecycle, state or message enums where that is expected and the exclusion stands once its sentence is corrected. Three are capability enums by the engine guide's own definition ("a reusable mathematical law, sensor/controller operation or animation primitive", `element-free-engine-design.md` Terms), and each dispatches in members that a new primitive extends:
- `AnimationKind` (`Clip`, `ExponentialFollow`, `Impulses`, `Oscillation`): `engine/presentation/AnimationBatch.cs:329` `StopPlayback`, `:430` `ValidateAdvance`, `:452` `EvaluateSlot` [20, logged at threshold 13]. Excluded as "animation evaluator options of one primitive".
- `AnimationImpulseOverlap` and `PresentationOverlap`: `engine/presentation/AnimationImpulseDefinition.cs:73` `Advance` (3 arms), `ElementPresentation.cs:262`, `:277`.
- `AnimationRepeat`: `AnimationEvaluation.cs:8` (3 arms).
Closing S6 on a list that leaves these out contradicts owner decision 24h ("every per-capability enum chain"). This is an inventory (S6b) and S6c matter, not a text change inside S6g, but S6g's claim "its publication closes story S6" is unsound until it is settled.

Fix (coordinator decision; default): assign `AnimationKind`, `AnimationImpulseOverlap`/`PresentationOverlap` and `AnimationRepeat` to S6c, which already owns `AnimationCurve`, `AnimationImpulseCurve` and `AnimationProperty`, and add the three to the "In scope" list and S6c's scope (or exclude each with a true, specific reason recorded in S6b). Correct the exclusion sentence to: "A member naming two or more of its values exists for N of these enums (the closing run reprints them); each is a lifecycle, state, message, cadence or closed-geometry enum whose members do not grow with a capability." Then S6g's text stands as written for G6/AC4 and adds: "the run log also lists, per excluded enum that the closing run prints with three or more members in one member, a one-line reason."
