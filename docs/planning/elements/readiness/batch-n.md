> ### Story ENGINE-THERMAL: Heat storage, transfer and phase change
> *Status: Backlog. Sprint key: `engine-thermal`. After the engine programme of owner decision 3a, ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK and ENGINE-LIQUID (owner decision 18: 3a programme → gas network → liquid → thermal → the rest).*
>
> *Present today:* a gas temperature inside the isobaric gas law only. *Missing:*
> - **Nodes.** A declared lumped thermal node on any body of a package: heat capacity, initial temperature (fixed, parameter-bound or selected by a Choice, including below ambient), ambient conductance and faces (a collider, or a declared disc aperture of one, each with a face conductance). Every liquid store is also a node with an authored starting temperature; inflow mixes by mass and outflow leaves at the store's temperature. A gas store is a node through the energy it carries. Units kelvin, J/K, W/K and W/(m·K) on parameters.
> - **One link law and one step.** Q̇ = G·ΔT on every link, applied each tick in compiled link order by the exact two-node exchange ΔQ = ΔT·C_r·(1 − e^(−G·dt ÷ C_r)), C_r = C_a·C_b ÷ (C_a + C_b) (C for a link to ambient); a node, liquid store or phase mass of zero capacity is inactive.
