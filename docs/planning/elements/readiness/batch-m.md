## 4. Gaps, with proposed text

Every story is stated element-free. Batch J's gaps 5 and 6 stand except where the unified ENGINE-FRACTURE text below replaces them.

1. **ENGINE-ASSEMBLY-JOINTS.** "Sockets carry a typed id and a role enum (mount, point); today `AnchorPointDescriptor.Name` is a string. A connection joins one mount to one point; other pairings are refused with a typed reason. The mount-carrying body and the point-carrying body of a connection do not collide, nor do bodies whose mounts share one point; all other pairs collide. The connect-time rule for a mount and point that are apart is fixed once; a made connection adds no impulse at Run start. **Acceptance:** a closed loop of three pinned members holds within 0.01 m for 10 s."
2. **`ENGINE-JOINT-KINDS`.** "A fixed joint holds a 2 m, 1.5 kg member carrying 4 kg at its free end (1.5 × 9.81 × 1 + 4 × 9.81 × 2 = 14.7 + 78.5 = 93.2 N·m at the root) within 0.02 m for 5 s. Every joint kind, and a supplied drive on a hinge, is qualified with a dynamic support body as well as a static one. The story settles whether a mechanical input may couple a coordinate that also carries a drive."
3. **`ENGINE-PARAMETER-BOUND-FRAMES`.** "A socket position and a revolute limit may each follow one declared Real parameter; a Choice parameter may select one declared value per option for a collider component or an aperture value." It is a prerequisite of GAP-07.
4. **ENGINE-FRACTURE (one text, reconciled with Batches J and L).** "Every load limit declares a measure from one closed set (axial tension; shear; bending moment; resultant; contact normal: the largest normal load the body carries from any one other body, summed over that pair's contact points; contact impulse: one substep's approach impulse, the delivered integrity observation) and a hold time of zero or more; the break occurs when the measure has exceeded the limit on every substep for the hold, and the settling transient at Run start does not count. A load of another kind or sign never breaks an attachment. A limit may be declared on an attachment or on a static or dynamic body; a body's break removes one declared collider or a declared set of its colliders (all of them for a whole-body break). Declared fragments are declared companion content of the package: body and collider rows of the same instance, fixed in count and compiled at Run, inactive until the break, each lying within the removed colliders' volume (checked at bundle validation); inactive rows take no part in the broadphase, sensors or the committed body set. A companion body may also take part from Run start. The substep in which a limit is exceeded is re-solved with the fragment rows active in place of the removed colliders, so the breaking contact acts on the fragments and momentum is conserved: each fragment starts at its declared pose in the parent's frame with the parent's rigid-body velocity at its own centre (rest for a static parent) and takes the parent's material values, payload role and per-body surface state. Mass: the fragments of a whole dynamic body sum to its mass; the fragments of a removed set sum to the mass that set carried, and the remaining body's post-break mass frame is compiled at Run with the rest; a static parent has no mass, so its fragments declare their own. A declaration that breaks these mass rules, places a fragment outside the removed volume, or has a non-finite or negative limit or hold is refused at bundle admission; no allocation during a tick; fragments break again only if they declare a limit. The break publishes the existing integrity-broken observation; binding an integrity observer to a gas store becomes optional (`CuriousContraptions.Abstractions/GasCouplingValues.cs:15`). Contact loads and impulses come from `ENGINE-CONTACT-OBSERVATIONS`. The committed load-to-limit ratio and the committed broken state are presentation drivers." A body break does not need player-made joints first. *Reconciliation:* this one text replaces Batch J's gaps 5 and 6 and Batch L's section 4 item 8. Batch L's "they take the parent's velocity" and this batch's "the parent's linear and angular velocity" are one rule, read as the parent's rigid-body velocity evaluated at each fragment's centre, so the fragments of a spinning parent keep its motion; the re-solved breaking substep (coordinator decision 16) then applies the breaking load to the fragments, and no intact body is left standing for a tick. Two clauses are new in this pass, not agreed text of either batch: the mass rule for a removed collider set and for a static parent, and the reading of fragment velocity at each fragment's centre. They are proposals for ENGINE-FRACTURE's author to confirm.
