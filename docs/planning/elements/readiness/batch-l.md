Each "decision" is a declared sensor feeding declared logic or memory nodes feeding a declared drive: a sighted walker is an optical receiver whose level closes a supply contact feeding a roller drive; the obstacle walker is two sprung bumper sliders whose endpoint contacts set and reset one memory node that selects between two ordinary drives; the missile is two receivers each gating one nose jet that turns the dart; a charge is a finite store released by a declared signal or by an integrity observer's occurrence. Cosmetic motion is presentation bindings on committed state. Reduced-form criteria that this costs are recorded as decisions (section 2, last rows).

2. **New story ENGINE-REACTION-THRUST** (`engine-reaction-thrust`; canonical text in `EL-090-steerable-blimp.md` section 3). "### Story ENGINE-REACTION-THRUST: Airflow source extensions: ambient reaction, converging flow, ambient angular drag. *Status: Backlog. Amends REQ-12. After ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES; every receiver and occluder candidate of an airflow source comes from ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES.* *Present today:* a conical airflow source on a dynamic frame receives the equal and opposite force and moment of what it delivers to each exposed receiver sample, paid from its store, supply or coordinate (`CuriousContraptions.tests/AirflowGeneralityTests.cs:189`). *Missing, in one change to the airflow source descriptor, its wire row and its read:* (a) **Ambient reaction.** A source may declare an ambient reaction: a reaction impulse on the frame body at the source origin, opposite to the axis, equal to the delivered law evaluated for the still ambient medium: (granted limit − impulse granted to exposed receivers) × pressure scale × clamp((flow speed + origin velocity · axis) ÷ flow speed, 0, 1). The work paid is that impulse × flow speed from the same finite input, for every input kind (store, supply consumer, coordinate). The reaction is applied at the source origin, so a source off the centre of mass turns its body. A source that does not declare it behaves exactly as today. The committed airflow read reports the ambient share. (b) **Flow sense.** A source declares a flow sense from a closed enum (Diverging, Converging). For a converging source the region is the same cone; the force on a sample is along the unit vector from the sample to the origin; the occlusion segment runs from the sample to the origin; caps, payment, pairing with the source frame and atmosphere scaling are unchanged.
