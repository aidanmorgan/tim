## 2. Optical conventions for other batches

Owned by [EL-176 section 10](../EL-176-red-laser-emitter.md#optical-conventions); cite that anchor by item number. They are no longer restated here: the copy that stood in this section until 23 November 2026 was a second home and had gone stale. Items marked there **(coordinator 16)** are settled by coordinator decision 16 (an earlier "(owner 16)" marker was a mis-citation). The amendments of 23 November 2026, made in EL-176 without renumbering, so that readers of earlier drafts can find them:

- **Item 2 (emitters).** Half-angle up to 180°; the payer is any finite conversion (a supply consumer, a declared store or a fuel burn; Batch N); granted energy not emitted is ledgered as a loss to a declared collider of the emitter, by default its first internal occluder (Batch O G17).
- **Item 3 (beam representation).** A Relay inlet declared as a receiver aperture takes its conserved share of diverging segments (Batch N); a diverging segment is multiplied by each Split's τ as well as each Transmit surface's, the Split's ρ share absorbed and ledgered, applied once per crossing at the entry face (Batch E (b)).
- **Item 5 (spatial query).** A segment that leaves a Relay outlet ignores the collider carrying that outlet at its origin (Batch E (c)).
- **Item 6 (what occludes).** The Fan's blades are decorative, turned by a presentation binding, and pass light by design (coordinator decision 25); the Fan's ring and post and the delivered rope remain visible-solid defects.
- **Item 8 (receivers).** A Relay inlet declared as a receiver aperture is among the receivers whose shares are conserved.
- **Item 9 (coupling).** Absorbed energy reaches a supply only by the store credit of coordinator decision 21 (coordinator decision 25; Batch E (a), Batch O): a declared fraction of the energy booked on the receiver's absorbing collider record, credited at the end of the solve to one declared finite store (one row of ENGINE-THERMAL; coordinator decision 26) that an ordinary source draws from, availability, where a part gates it, by a threshold node. The next-tick allowance form is withdrawn.
- **Item 7 (surface responses).** A Relay outlet may declare its own range (TH-06) and may be declared relative to the inlet hit point and incoming direction (RAD-14).
