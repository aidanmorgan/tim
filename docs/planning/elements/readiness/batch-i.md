> ### ENGINE-TENSILE-EXTENSIONS, cutting clause: blade pairs that sever tensile spans or seams by declared cutting work
> *Owner decision 3a names the story; coordinator decision 16 makes this the one cutting-work law. After ENGINE-CONNECTION-MATERIALS.*
>
> *Present today:* a commanded release of a tensile span that removes a route's tensile rows at a committed step. *Missing:* a declared blade pair: a fixed straight edge on one body and a straight edge on a body moving on one revolute coordinate, both through the hinge axis, with a blade thickness along that axis and a capacity C (J, constant or parameter-bound). A span whose segment crosses the blade plane within the thickness, between the edges and inside their lengths engages when the perpendicular gap between the edges at the crossing point falls to the material's strand diameter d. While engaged and closing it opposes the moving edge with force R/d at the crossing point and never pushes the blades apart. If R is at most C the route is severed when the gap reaches 0, having absorbed R; if R exceeds C the gap is held at d. The absorbed work leaves the mechanism and is a committed read on the cutter. Candidate spans are found through a bounded query, not a scan of every route each tick.
>
> **Targets and solid strands** (from `readiness/batch-l.md` section 4 item 11). A cutting target is a tensile span or an attachment seam: a declared straight segment with thickness d and resistance R on a body held by a fixed joint (ENGINE-JOINT-KINDS); severing a seam releases its fixed joint one-way and Reset restores it. Both edges load the target equally. Blade-edge colliders do not collide with a target's strand or seam piece; the cutting force stands in for that contact. Candidate targets come from ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES.
>
> *Unblocks:* EL-066 Scissors, EL-068 Tin snips and EL-078 Can opener.
>
> **Specific acceptance:**
> - **Given** a span crossing a blade pair at distance x from the pivot, **when** the closing coordinate is given torque just below and just above R·x/d, **then** only the second severs the route, the cutter's committed cut-work read equals R, and the mechanism's energy falls by R.
> - **And** a material whose R exceeds the cutter's capacity holds the gap at d for any input, and a span outside the blade thickness or beyond the edges is never engaged.
> - **And** a second declared material is cut by its own d and R with no engine code naming either; Reset restores the route.
> - **And** a seam and a span of equal R and d are cut by the same blade pair at the same torque.
