## 2. Liquid conventions: LG1 to LG8 and the additions A1 to A9

### LG1 to LG8 (adopted into EL-001)

EL-001 line 45 maps them: LG1 = L5 and L6; LG2 = L3 and L7; LG3, LG4, LG5 = L4, L11, L12; LG6 = L15; LG7 = L12; LG8 = L13. The long wording stays here, as EL-001 says, now in its current form (the amendments of EL-001 and coordinator decision 21 are written in, not beside):

| # | Addition | Extends | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| LG1 Immersed mouth | A mouth may be declared immersed instead of joinable: a fluid port of the one record with no socket. Its node is resolved by the innermost interior that contains its point (A1), the same query as a depth probe, and it is wet while that store's surface is above the point. In no interior, a Discharge or Bidirectional immersed mouth is a free end (L8) and an Intake one passes nothing. It declares an aperture area and, with its store, is one aperture (L7). It may sit on a static or a dynamic body. Cost: one point query per immersed mouth per substep, resolved once at Run when mouth and store are both static | L5; L6 unchanged | EL-023, EL-024, EL-029, EL-035 |
| LG2 Crested fitting | A fitting may declare a crest: a crest datum, a prime volume V_p and a self-priming rate (default 0). It declares no lift limit (coordinator decision 21): the lift is bounded by its intake leg, because the intake uncovers first. It holds a prime inventory p in [0, V_p], which is liquid in the ledger. While p < V_p it conducts nothing between its end mouths. p gains from liquid delivered into a mouth declared to open into the prime inventory; from the store at its Intake end at the self-priming rate while that mouth is wet; and from ordinary flow while the head at the Intake end is at or above the crest datum. Liquid arriving when p = V_p passes on to the Discharge end. While p = V_p the fitting conducts by L7, exempt from the crest rule. The column breaks in the step in which the Intake mouth is dry: p leaves through the Discharge end and the fitting is closed again. Committed observation: prime fraction | L3, L7 | EL-024 (rate 0), EL-035 (rate above 0) |
| LG3 Compliant sealed store | A store may be declared sealed and compliant with a head at full, H_e. It has no open aperture, no interior (A6), no overflow and no tilt loss. The total head at each of its mouths is the mouth elevation + H_e·V/C; at V = C it accepts nothing | L4 | EL-033 |
| LG4 Capacity bound to a coordinate | A store's capacity may be a clamped linear map of a committed prismatic or revolute coordinate, with parameter terms. When the map lowers the capacity below the volume held, the excess is pending overflow and leaves in the next substep (L9) through the store's declared relief mouth (A8), else its overflow point. The liquid exerts no force on the coordinate, except L12 c's intake-head force ρ·g·h·A on a prismatic coordinate whose store takes liquid in through a mouth under head (EL-229) | L4, L9; the store-side twin of L14 b | EL-168, EL-171 |
| LG5 Porous store | A store may be declared porous. It keeps its contents at every orientation (L11 does not apply). Its interior is its carrying collider (A6). It may declare an immersion uptake rate k: while its carrying body is immersed (L12 a) in another store, liquid moves from that store into it at k × the body's submerged fraction, never past its capacity and never more than the other store holds | L3, L11, L12 | EL-034, EL-171 |
| LG6 Signed work-target input | A work target's coordinate input declares a sense. Only motion in that sense is input and meets the resisting law. Motion the other way absorbs nothing and adds no head; the edge is then open, as any ungranted work target is (coordinator decision 16) | L15 | EL-023 |
| LG7 Displacement envelope | A body may declare displacement shapes (boxes, spheres or cylinders in its frame) that stand in for all its colliders in L12 a. Buoyancy, damping and the displaced volume added to the level use the part of the envelope below the surface; the displaced volume is that of the union of the shapes (A3); the region inside the envelope is not part of a surrounding store's interior (A2). Without a declaration the colliders are the displacement shapes | L12 a | EL-029; EL-014 (Batch F) |
| LG8 Cumulative edge volume | For a fitting that declares it, the committed cumulative volume that has passed in the declared direction since Run began, kept with the bulk and remainder pair. Flow against the direction changes nothing. Reset zeroes it. Readable by presentation drivers, threshold nodes and goals | L13 | EL-031 |

No Batch G part declares a Source node, so `Supplied` stays zero in every Batch G scenario. Liquid acts on bodies only through L12 a and L12 b.
