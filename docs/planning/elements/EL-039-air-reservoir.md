### Behaviour and numbers

- The store obeys P1: pressure is derived from mass, volume and energy. Gas leaves or enters only through a connected port; a tank with nothing connected keeps its charge for the whole Run.
- Two tanks of equal volume joined by a line settle at the mean of their pressures, because total internal energy p·V/(γ − 1) is conserved. With a hose between them the settled pressure is Σ(p·V) / ΣV over both tanks and the line (4.0 × 10⁻⁴ m³, [EL-038](EL-038-pneumatic-hose.md)), and the line starts at the pressure of the tank on its Output end (F9). A default tank (400 000 Pa) joined to an ambient default tank settles at 253 600 Pa: (4000 + 160 + 1013.25) J over 0.0204 m³. A 0.002 m³ tank at 600 000 Pa joined to a 0.05 m³ ambient tank settles at 124 200 Pa: (1200 + 240 + 5066.25) J over 0.0524 m³. Neither value depends on temperature.
- Thermal: the store is a thermal node under thermal T2 through the energy its gas carries, declared with ambient conductance 0 and no faces, so it is adiabatic within a Run by declaration, not by a second rule (question 3 below).

### Engine conformance (22 November 2026)

Checked against the owner's conformance instruction of 22 November 2026, the element-free engine design (`_bmad/custom/element-free-engine-design.md`, contracts D1–D7) and the compilation model (`docs/gpu-f32-physics.md#compilation-model`).

1. **Declaration data only.** The package is one declaration class, one scene and one icon. Its behaviour is one static body, three colliders, one contact material, one rigid gas store, two fluid ports, two parameters and one presentation binding. 2. **Generic laws.** Pressure is the ideal-gas state of a declared store (P1). Gas moves by the one nozzle law through declared lines (P6, F5, F9). The rating is the upper limit of the `initial_pressure` parameter and the dial's full scale; the charging stop belongs to the compressor's declared rating. Contact uses the general contact law with the declared material. The family rules F1–F11 are statements about the general gas network, never about this part.
3. **Three layers.**
   - *Colliders (physics graph):* `col_` shapes on body 0 from the admitted set: tank cylinder, foot box, dial boss cylinder; static material as declared. Physics reads no art.
   - *Visuals (rendering graph):* the art table above. `Tank`, `Foot`, `BandA`, `BandB`, `Dial/Boss` and `Dial/Face` are not moved by anything (a static body's pose). No art node is a collider.

4. **One-way flow and typing.** Physics → animation → renderer. Parameter keys, port ids, port roles and the line end are enums; pressures, volumes and masses are f32; strings appear only in the declaration's player text and at save boundaries.
5. **Flat-state counts per instance.** Bodies 1 (static); colliders 3 (2 cylinders, 1 box); joints 0; drives 0; sensors 0; domain nodes 1 (one gas store node); ports 2; stores 1 (gas); presentation bindings 1; parameters 2. All counts are fixed at compile time.
6. **Performance fitness.** Three primitive colliders, the same number as the delivered Battery (`elements/Battery/battery.tscn:99`–`:111`); the boss is there because the dial is a proud visible solid, not for decoration. The body is static, so it joins no island and needs nothing from `ENGINE-ISLANDS-SLEEP`. The store update is constant work per store and each line is constant work per tick; nothing scales with the scene and the part makes no spatial query. Store, line and path tables are sized at Run from the compiled declarations, with no fixed scene ceiling (design contract D5); their row layout is `ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK`'s, inside the flat tables of `ENGINE-SOA-SIMD`, and no allocation happens in a tick (`ENGINE-TICK-ALLOCATION`).
7. **Tests are scenarios.** Rows A1–A9 run the production simulation on authored constructions and assert committed pressures, masses, links and presentation reads. None needs a code path that exists only for this part.

## 6. Open questions

1. **Game volume versus art volume.** The default 0.01 m³ is the legacy fixture volume, far smaller than the 0.42 m³ tank art. Whether the declared volume should follow the art or stay a tuned game value. **Resolved (D1, D13):** the declared volume is a tuned game value with default 0.01 m³ and the art does not scale with it, because charge and discharge times must fit a lesson and size is fixed per kind.
2. **Gas connection direction.** The current port model has only Input and Output; a reservoir both fills and drains. Two same-node sockets (proposed) versus a bidirectional gas port. **Resolved under the standing delegation:** two sockets on one store, `GasIn` (Input) and `GasOut` (Output), with flow direction set by pressure (F3). It keeps the delivered Output-to-Input link rule (`BundlePortsTests.AWrongDomainOrOutputToOutputLinkIsStillRefused`) and the supply-side to consumer-side reading of every other connection.
3. **Thermal coupling.** Adiabatic compression heats the charge (about 480 K at 600 000 Pa from 288 K); whether the tank cools toward ambient (needs S543 conduction) or stays adiabatic. **Resolved (D19):** the tank is adiabatic within a Run and returns to its authored temperature on Reset. Adiabatic is a declaration: the store is a thermal node (thermal T2) with ambient conductance 0 and no faces. Cooling toward ambient belongs to `ENGINE-THERMAL` and is added only if a lesson uses it.
4. **Category.** A new `Air` drawer category versus reusing Power. **Resolved:** `ElementCategory.Power`, because `Air` is not in the closed enum and the Fan and Windmill are already in Power.

**Coordinator decisions under the owner's standing delegation (22 November 2026).** They settle the family contract once, here; the other pneumatic specs cite them.

5. **Hose start pressure (F9).** A line starts at the pressure of the store on its Output end, else of the store on its Input end, else at ambient. Reason: the delivered gas law (one isobaric store per body, `CuriousContraptions.Abstractions/IsobaricGas.cs:7`) has no lines, so the rule is chosen for the planned conserved-transfer law, whose acceptance says a closed valve transfers none. Starting every line at ambient would make a charged tank lose 3% at Run start to its own closed hose and make a gauge read 388 500 Pa on a 400 000 Pa tank. Every settled value in the family was recomputed under F9.
6. **A gauge that can be built (F3, F11).** A Fluid Output takes several hoses. A tee part and a pass-through gauge port were both rejected as larger: each adds a declaration, and the delivered link record already admits several links on one port (G13).
7. **`todo-426` kept in full.** Spring return on exhaust, repeated strokes and exhaust to a whistle are delivered through [EL-041](EL-041-pneumatic-directional-valve.md), which gains an `Exhaust` Fluid Output. The release valve stays a one-shot latch. No requirement criterion is weakened (row A9, EL-041 rows D-A14 and D-A15).
8. **Units and links.** Pressure stays pascals absolute with ambient 101 325 Pa, and links stay Output to Input (F1–F3). The pistons EL-230 and EL-231 (new-identities batch) have since adopted pascals absolute and 101 325 Pa (`readiness/new-identities.md`); their port names follow F3: EL-230's one chamber inlet is `GasIn`, and EL-231's two are `ExtendIn` and `RetractIn`, named by the chamber each serves (the earlier `ChamberPort`, `ExtendPort` and `RetractPort` are withdrawn).
9. **Hose constants (F10).** Line volume and throat are declared data of the connection kind, not engine constants.
10. **Dial.** The dial is a solid boss with a collider, not a print on the end cap, because the +X end cap carries the `GasOut` socket.

Needs the owner: none. The hose is a solid strand by owner decision 1 ([EL-038](EL-038-pneumatic-hose.md)); it does not block this spec.
