## Declaration

### Liquid family conventions (stated once, here)

Each rule is a statement about the general liquid network that `ENGINE-LIQUID` must deliver from declared data. None names a part.

| # | Convention |
| --- | --- |
| L1 Units | Volume m³; mass kg; flow m³/s; elevation, depth and head m; aperture area m²; pressure Pa absolute; power W; energy J; time s of game time (D10). Ambient pressure is 101 325 Pa, the delivered Standard boundary (`CuriousContraptions.Abstractions/AtmosphereBoundary.cs:12`). Litres are not used anywhere, including player readouts (m³ to two decimals, or to two significant figures where that shows more, so a 0.00125 m³ kettle does not read 0.00). |
| L2 Liquid | The liquid is one declared material row, "Water": density ρ = 16 kg/m³ ([D2](../invest/delivery-batches.md#3-family-level-decisions)), immersion damping c = 1.0 s⁻¹, discharge coefficient Cd = 0.6. Other capabilities (heat, radiation) attach their own rows to it. It is incompressible. Every store is also a thermal node with an authored starting temperature (owner decision 20; [TH-04 section 11](TH-04-electrical-heating-plate.md#thermal-conventions), T2): its capacity is the liquid mass × 1046 J/(kg·K) (T11), its starting temperature defaults to the ambient 288 K (T1), inflow mixes by mass and outflow leaves at the store's temperature; the node is `ENGINE-THERMAL`'s and no liquid rule reads temperature. **Heat moves with liquid (coordinator decision 26):** in the liquid step, per substep, every transfer of volume ΔV carries ΔV × the source store's enthalpy per unit volume H ÷ V; a fitting passes the mixed enthalpy per unit volume of its inflows; a stream credits its enthalpy with its volume; spilled and drained volume carries its enthalpy into that account (evaporated mass carries its own by T5). This reads no temperature and, with L7's bound ΔV ≤ V, never makes H negative. `ENGINE-THERMAL` adds this column to the liquid step; its per-tick thermal step reads H as the last substep committed it (thermal T2, T8). Gravity is the one uniform-field law (coordinator decision 16, `ENGINE-FIELD-FORCE`), g = 9.81 m/s² (`elements/PoweredGate/PoweredGateElementDeclaration.cs:83`), with buoyancy scaled by the same field. Hydrostatic gradient ρ·g = 156.96 Pa per metre. Marks and defaults use 2⁻⁴ m³ = 0.0625 m³ = 1 kg. A Real volume parameter is continuous, because `ParameterDescriptor` has no step (`CuriousContraptions.Abstractions/ParameterDescriptor.cs:52`); a part that needs whole steps declares a Choice. |
| L3 Nodes | A package declares liquid nodes; Run compiles them to flat rows. **Store**: a finite volume with a free surface. **Fitting**: the sealed interior of a part, zero volume; a fitting may declare a crest with a small prime inventory and a self-priming rate; it declares no lift limit, because its intake leg bounds the lift (L7; Batch G's LG2; A9, coordinator decision 21). **Sink**: an accounted boundary that only receives. **Source**: an accounted boundary that only supplies at a declared rate; no water part in either batch declares one. Four world accounts, in m³: `Spilled`, `Drained`, `Supplied`, and `Evaporated` (the volume m ÷ ρ that leaves a free surface by a thermal evaporation row, T5; coordinator decision 26). A declared phase row between a liquid store and a gas store (boiling and condensing, T5) moves volume between the store and that gas store's ledger and is counted in `Drained` (liquid leaving) and `Supplied` (liquid arriving); evaporation to the world is counted in `Evaporated` only, never in `Drained`. |
| L4 Store | Declares capacity C (m³), plan area A (m²), interior height H, its interior extents, and one or more floor datum points in its body frame. V stays in [0, C]. **Interior**: the open aperture's region extruded down to the floor datum plane; immersion, probes, stream credit and other capabilities read liquid only inside it. Where declared interiors nest, a stream sample, a probe and an immersed port resolve to the innermost interior containing the point (A1). **Level, one relation for every pose**: for interior width w along the tilt, breadth b = A/w, tilted by θ from upright, with V_l = V + V_displaced (L12): depth normal to the floor at the low edge is d = √(2·V_l·tan θ / b) while d ≤ w·tan θ, otherwise d = V_l/A + (w·tan θ)/2. Surface elevation = elevation of the lowest floor datum + d·cos θ. At θ = 0 this is d = V_l/A. **Full** means V = C. **Empty** means V = 0. A tapered vessel declares the equivalent area C ÷ H. Variants by declaration: a sealed compliant store (no aperture, no overflow; mouth head = mouth elevation + H_e·V/C; LG3); a porous store that keeps its contents at every pose (LG5); a capacity bound to a committed coordinate by a clamped linear map (LG4). A porous store's interior is its declared carrying collider, or a declared interior box where the porous medium is enclosed art; a sample that ends on that collider's aperture face, or inside that box, is credited. A sealed store declares no interior: nothing is immersed in it, probed in it or streamed into it (A6). **Closed store.** A store with no open aperture (L9) is closed: at V = C its mouths pass flow as a lossless fitting (L3, L7) and it never overflows. While V < C it is an ordinary store: a free surface, the level relation above, wet and dry mouths; its interior is its declared interior extents above the floor datum, which probes and immersed mouths read, and no stream reaches it because it has no aperture. At V = C, while its mouths would bring in more than they take out, it holds V = C and passes the flow through as a fitting whose openings sum to zero; once outflow exceeds inflow it is a store again. It holds no pending overflow and has no tilt excess (L11), because it has no rim. "No open aperture" is not the sealed variant (LG3): a sealed store has no free surface and no interior, and its mouth head is mouth elevation + H_e·V/C at every fill. |
| L5 Mouth | A liquid mouth is a fluid port of the one fluid port contract (coordinator decision 16; owner `ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK`): a node, a role, an aperture area and a socket. Sockets are of two kinds, **mouth-joined** (a scene mouth joined by adjacency) and **link-joined** (a terminal joined by a connection); both compile to the same network edge and no join rule depends on the medium. Water parts use mouth-joined sockets only. Mouths are declared snap sockets with package-local ids (`ENGINE-CAPABILITY-INSTANCES`, which replaces the two-ended `TubeEnd`); a part declares one or more. Ids follow flow: `Start` upstream, `End` downstream, `Branch` a third. Each socket declares its bore, capture distance and capture angle; the water kit declares 0.20 m and 20°. The socket position is the mouth's head datum. Roles: Intake (inward only), Discharge (outward only), Bidirectional. Two bores: **pipe bore radius 0.10 m** (declared area A₀ = 0.031416 m², outer radius 0.14 m) and **channel bore 0.20 m** (an open channel 0.40 m wide × 0.20 m deep; declared area 0.08 m², not the circle of its bore). A store mouth is **wet** while the surface is above its datum; a dry store mouth supplies nothing and can still receive. A mouth may instead be declared **immersed** (LG1): it joins no mouth. An immersed mouth is a fluid port of the one record with no socket; its node is resolved by the innermost interior that contains its point (A1), the same query as a depth probe (A7). An immersed mouth whose point lies inside no interior (a hull resting on a floor with its port below the floor datum plane, or a vessel lifted clear of every store) is sealed: it passes nothing in either direction until its point lies inside an interior again. It is never a free end (L8), because only a declared outlet or an unjoined socket is one. A store mouth may lie below its store's floor datum: its head is the store's surface elevation while the store holds liquid and its own datum when the store is empty (A7). |
| L6 Join | Two mouths join only by the authoring snap: a mouth within its declared capture distance and angle of a free mouth of equal bore snaps, and two mouths are joined when they coincide within 10⁻⁴ m and face opposite (`engine/TubeMouthSnap.cs:27`–`:28`). D3 is read as "no second way to join two mouths"; an immersed mouth (L5) reaches a store without joining a mouth. At Run compile each joined pair becomes one network edge. A joined mouth is occupied. Joins are fixed for the Run. Different bores never join (ball pipe bore radius 0.65 m, pipe bore radius 0.10 m, channel bore 0.20 m). There is no Water wire domain and no hose. |
| L7 Flow law | Head is lost only at **apertures**: (i) a store mouth (a joined pair of store mouths is one aperture, with the smaller area); (ii) a free end of a fitting (L8); (iii) a declared seat or outlet (valve, one-way seat, nozzle exit, leak hole, gate), each one aperture with its declared area. Each passes Q = K·√Δh toward the lower total head, K = Cd·A·√(2g). Aperture area does not vary with submergence (a game simplification). Pipe bore K₀ = 0.6 × 0.031416 × 4.42945 = **0.08349 m^2.5/s**; channel bore K_c = 0.6 × 0.08 × 4.42945 = **0.21261**. Fitting interiors and fitting-to-fitting joins are lossless; at every fitting the flows sum to zero. Series: 1/K² = Σ 1/K_i². Total head at a wet store mouth is the store's surface elevation; at a dry store mouth and at a free end, the datum elevation. **Crest rule:** liquid passes a mouth datum only while the highest total head among the wet stores feeding that fitting, plus the head added by granted work targets between that store and the datum, is at or above the datum; a crested fitting whose prime inventory is full is exempt (LG2; A9, coordinator decision 21). So a route of kit pipes cannot carry liquid over a point above its supply surface unless a granted pump or screw lifts it, and the Siphon obeys the same law with a prime. **Bounded, conservative step:** (a) a store never gives more than it holds, and a store in the wedge regime of L4 never receives more in a step than raises its surface to the supplying head; (b) a transfer between two stores never passes the amount that would level them; (c) fittings with more than two openings are solved by a declared fixed number of sweeps, after which each fitting's outflows are scaled to its inflows, so a fitting never stores or creates volume and the unconverged remainder shows only as a flow error inside the 10% tolerance. **Stability argument for `ENGINE-LIQUID` to confirm:** for two stores joined by one aperture the level difference Δ changes per substep by c·√Δ with c = K·(1/A₁ + 1/A₂)/480; a step overshoots only when Δ < c², and then by at most c²/4. Examples: channel bore between two 1 m gutters, c² = 4.9 × 10⁻⁶ m; pipe bore between two 0.25 m² stores, c² = 1.9 × 10⁻⁶ m. The smallest declared store sets the worst case: TH-18's 0.02 m² sump on a pipe-bore mouth gives c = 0.08349 × 50 ÷ 480 = 0.0087 and c² = 7.6 × 10⁻⁵ m against a large store, and c² = 3.0 × 10⁻⁴ m between two such stores, still 33 times under the 0.01 m tolerance. The substep is 1/480 s at every cadence, so these figures do not depend on dt. In the wedge regime the effective area falls to zero with volume, so c is unbounded and clamps (a) and (b) carry the bound. For coupled fittings the scaling in (c) carries conservation; convergence within the declared sweeps is the story's to measure. |
| L8 Free end and stream | An unjoined Discharge or Bidirectional mouth, and a declared outlet that is not a mouth, is a free end. It passes liquid outward only, while the head behind it exceeds its datum elevation. An unjoined Intake mouth passes nothing. The liquid leaves as a **stream** with start velocity Q/(Cd·A) along the outlet normal plus the owning body's velocity there; then gravity only. A stream is not an inventory. It is a fixed number of sample paths, declared by `ENGINE-LIQUID`, spread across its contracted section (radius √Cd × the aperture's equivalent radius), each carrying an equal share of the step's volume. A sample that ends inside a store's or sink's declared interior (the innermost, where interiors nest; A1), on the liquid surface, the floor or any collider there, is credited to that node in the same step. A sample that meets any other collider, the bench or the world bound first is credited to `Spilled`. No collider is exempt by kind: a rope, a bail or an arm above a vessel costs its shadow, and a float inside a vessel costs nothing. A stream exerts no force on a body in this convention (L12). Streams are drawn by the shared liquid presentation from committed stream rows. |
| L9 Open aperture, overflow, spill | A store or sink may declare one open aperture: a bounded flat region (rectangle or disc) in its body frame with an upward normal; it is the top of the interior (L4). Each substep is one pass: aperture flows from committed heads, then stream credits. Volume that would take V + V_displaced above the retained capacity C(θ) (L11) is held as that store's **pending overflow**: it is in the store's ledger volume, is never read as head, and leaves from the declared overflow point in the next substep as a stream under L8, with no discharge speed of its own: its start velocity is the owning body's velocity at that point, so a moving vessel's pour carries the vessel's motion. L8's stream rule governs every stream, overflow, tilt excess (L11) and relief included; this item states only where and when overflow leaves. A cascade therefore advances one vessel per substep (1/480 s) and the work per substep stays bounded. A store with a bound capacity may declare a **relief mouth**: its pending overflow enters the joined node in the next substep, or leaves as a free end if unjoined. A relief mouth passes only pending overflow, never head-driven flow (A8). **Spill** means liquid credited to `Spilled`; it is terminal. |
| L10 Ledger | At every committed tick: Σ store volumes (pending overflow and prime inventories included) + `Spilled` + `Drained` + `Evaporated` = Σ authored initial volumes + `Supplied`. Stores use the engine's bulk and remainder pair (`docs/gpu-f32-physics.md:132`). |
| L11 Tilt | The retained capacity C(θ) is the volume at d = H in L4: C·(1 − (w/(2H))·tan θ) while tan θ ≤ H/w, else C·H/(2·w·tan θ). The excess is pending overflow (L9) and leaves from the lowest rim point. A porous store (LG5) and a closed store (L4) are exempt. |
| L12 Bodies | (a) **Immersion.** A dynamic body's displacement shapes below the surface inside a store's interior receive buoyancy ρ·g·V_sub upward at the centroid of the submerged part and damping −c·m·(V_sub/V_body)·v. The displacement shapes are the body's colliders unless it declares a **displacement envelope** (boxes, spheres or cylinders in its frame; LG7), which a hollow vessel does so that it floats by its outline. A body's displaced volume is the volume of the union of its displacement shapes; overlapping shapes are counted once (A3). The region inside a body's declared displacement envelope is excluded from the interior of any store that surrounds it: shapes inside it are not immersed in that store, and liquid does not enter it except through the body's own declared seats, mouths and apertures (A2). V_sub adds to the level (L4) and to the overflow test (L9). This displaced-volume law is buoyancy in liquid only; buoyancy in air is a body's declared ambient lift, and no convention states a displaced-air law (coordinator decision 28). c = 1.0 s⁻¹ is a starting value (D1): `ENGINE-LIQUID` sets it for a damping ratio near 0.5 at a floating draught, because stable rest is an acceptance target. A porous store on an immersed body may take liquid up at a declared rate (LG5). (b) **Contents.** A store on a dynamic body adds ρ·V to that body's mass at the centroid of the liquid region given by L4 at the committed pose, with no added inertia (A5, coordinator decision 21). This is a new mass-source kind: the delivered `BodyMassSourceKind.CenteredInventory` (`CuriousContraptions.Abstractions/BodyMassSourceDeclaration.cs:10`, `:20`) adds a gas inventory at the body's centre only, and with contents at the centre no vessel could tip or right itself by its contents. (c) Liquid exerts no other force on a body beyond (a), (b) and (d): no jet force, no hydrostatic load on a gate, valve arm or coordinate, except two rows of `ENGINE-LIQUID`. A declared capture region may do paid work on a coordinate (Batch F E-7). A store whose capacity is bound to a prismatic coordinate (LG4) receives force ρ·g·h·A along that coordinate, where h is the head at its intake mouth and A the volume its capacity map gains per metre of travel, paid by the volume entering through that mouth at that head (EL-229). A bound store that takes liquid in through no mouth under head (stream credits through an aperture, porous uptake) receives none (EL-171). (d) **Buoyancy reaction.** Buoyancy and immersion damping on a body immersed in a store that a dynamic body carries are reacted on the carrying body at the same point (A4): a carried vessel weighs the same whether a body in it rests on its floor or floats in its contents, and a body resting in a floating hull is buoyed only by the liquid in the hull. |
| L13 Observations | Committed per tick and readable by presentation drivers, threshold signal nodes and goals: per store volume, depth and fill fraction; per aperture signed flow; per node pressure (Pa absolute, 101 325 + ρ·g × depth below its head); per account total; seat opening; probe depth; prime fraction; and, for a fitting that declares it, cumulative volume passed in a declared direction (LG8). A gas node's pressure is never equated with a liquid node's (L15). |
| L14 Aperture control | A seat's opening s in [0, 1] scales its area. s comes from declared data only: (a) a parameter, including one value per option of a Choice; (b) a committed revolute or prismatic coordinate through a clamped linear map with parameter terms; (c) a signal memory node, one declared s per state; (d) any committed scalar observation through a clamped linear map. | L15 Work target and gas edge | A declared edge may add head from paid work: hydraulic power ρ·g·Q·h_p never exceeds efficiency × the input power, and h_p never exceeds a declared maximum. One input rule for every supply: the energy taken is the hydraulic work done ÷ efficiency; an electrical input debits that from its grant (`ENGINE-SUPPLY-DEMAND`); a shaft input meets a resisting torque of that power ÷ |ω|, never above a declared torque cap or rating ÷ |ω|, in a declared sense of rotation only (LG6); **a gas input** debits it from the gas store's internal energy, and the edge adds no head while that store's pressure does not exceed the liquid node's head pressure at the edge (coordinator decision 26). ### Behaviour and numbers

- **Depletion.** With its mouth free the tank drains through one aperture: dV/dt = −K₀·√d with V = A·d, so the time to empty from depth d₀ is 2·A·√d₀ / K₀ = 2 × 1 × 1 / 0.08349 = **23.95 s**, and the volume left after time t is (1 − t/23.95)². At 12 s: (1 − 0.501)² = 0.249 m³.
- **Head weakens flow.** Flow at depth 1.0 m is 0.0835 m³/s; at 0.25 m it is 0.0417 m³/s: half.
- **Two tanks.** Two tanks placed front to front (mouths joined, one aperture) with 1.0 m³ and 0 settle at 0.5 m³ each. The level difference Δ obeys dΔ/dt = −2·K₀·√Δ / A, so it closes in √Δ₀ / K₀ = 1 / 0.08349 = **12.0 s**.
- **Pressure.** Full: 101 325 + 156.96 × 1.0 = 101 482 Pa at the floor.
- **Displacement.** A body lowered into a full tank spills its displaced volume from the front rim one substep later (L9).
- **Why these values.** A 1 m³ tank is Receiver scale and holds 16 marks; draining in tens of seconds keeps a pour inside a lesson. An unjoined mouth empties the tank, so a level that wants a tank to hold places a cap, a tap or a join on it.
