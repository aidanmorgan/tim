### Behaviour and numbers

- **Law.** The optical transport law carries a collimated segment to the pane; the Transmit response multiplies each channel by its declared factor, P′ = τ ∘ P; what is not transmitted is absorbed by the pane and ledgered (not heat until ENGINE-THERMAL, decision K-10). The part adds no law.
- **Amber through red.** (10, 7.8, 3.2) W × (1, 0, 0) = (10, 0, 0) W; the pane absorbs 7.8 + 3.2 = 11 W, which is the declared loss (K-5). A red receiver then reads strength 10 W ≥ its 2.5 W threshold (0.25 legacy units) with purity 10 / 10 = 100 %; without the filter amber has red purity 10 / 21 = 47.6 % and fails the 90 % rule. That contrast is the lesson (decision D18). The amber Laser is the mixed-channel beam of the collimated rows; the Flashlight, the white source, is delivered before this slice (Story 13.1) and its cone is filtered in row Fc.
- **No gain, no recolour.** Two red filters: (10, 0, 0) × (1, 0, 0) = (10, 0, 0) W, never more than the input. Light with no red, for example blue-filtered amber (0, 0, 3.2) W: × (1, 0, 0) = (0, 0, 0); the segment ends at the pane.
- **Unlike stack.** Red then green: (10, 0, 0) × (0, 1, 0) = (0, 0, 0). Either order extinguishes.
- **Whole pane in a square opening.** The opening is 1.3 m square and the pane fills it (half-width 0.65 m). A ray 0.60 m above centre is filtered, and so is a ray through a corner at (0.60, 0.60), 0.85 m from the centre. A ray 0.74 m above centre meets the top rail (0.65 to 0.83 m) and is absorbed.
- **Cone light.** A Flashlight cone is a terminal diverging segment: it starts no further segment. A transmitting surface between the cone and a receiver sample multiplies it ([optical conventions](EL-176-red-laser-emitter.md#optical-conventions), item 3), so a sample seen through this pane receives only the passed channel; a sample behind the frame or base is in shadow. The Flashlight emits (30, 30, 30) W over a 15° half-angle (0.2141 sr): 140.1 W/sr per channel. With its lens face 3 m from a Light receiver (CAT-038: disc 0.950 m², one centre sample) the axis irradiance is 140.1 ÷ 9 = 15.57 W/m² per channel and the receiver's share is 0.950 × 15.57 = 14.8 W per channel; through this pane it reads (14.8, 0, 0) W. A receiver's cone reading is its share A × Σ wᵢEᵢ; when the shares of one diverging segment summed over all receivers exceed its power, every share is scaled by power ÷ sum (conventions item 8; coordinator decision 16). In row Fc one receiver stands in the cone and its unfiltered share, 44.4 W, is below the cone's 90 W, so nothing is scaled. At 1.5 m the cone's radius is 1.5 × tan 15° = 0.40 m, inside the pane's 0.65 m half-width, so the frame shades no sample.
- **Solid pane.** The pane is a box collider: every ball stops at it. It blocks the pipe bore, 1.3 m across, completely.
- **Budgets.** A filter does not change direction or remaining range: with the Laser's 16 m range, a detector 15.5 m along the path is lit through the filter and one 16.5 m along is not. Each passage uses one of the 16 interactions a path may have.
- **Tick order.** A filter holds no state. One optical and radiation solve per committed tick of duration dt, after the last substep and before the thermal step and commit (the [combined tick order](EL-176-red-laser-emitter.md#combined-tick-order), optical convention 4; coordinator decisions 16 and 26). It reads the energy granted to each emitter over this tick's substeps, this tick's final collider poses and signal levels as resolved at the start of this tick. Segments, readings and poses commit together; readings act from the next tick. No row asserts a tick except where a next-tick decision is the feature. No optical phase is delivered today.

Tests run the production simulation through the Workshop harness.

**Tolerances (the one optical set; coordinator decision 21).** Power readings, beam or cone, ± 2 %: the smallest declared loss step is 5 % (one mirror), so 2 % tells n lossy parts from n ± 1, and a missing 0.9 or 0.5 factor is at least 10 %; a wrong factor flips a detector, a gate or the Solar panel's supply. "Zero" behind an extinguishing pane is the same bound, below 2 % of the entering power, so a pane that leaks a channel fails. Stall guard 1 s of game time: it excludes a light that never arrives; the response itself is one tick. Segment end points ± 0.02 m: under 5 % of the smallest aperture radius (0.43 m) and below the 0.03 m edge clearance of the gate rows, so a path that would clip or miss an aperture fails. Exact: authored Reset values and the absence of any segment or reading after Reset (intentional zero). Threshold cases sit at least 5 % from the threshold and assert state only.
