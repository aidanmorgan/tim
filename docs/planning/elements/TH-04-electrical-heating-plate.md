## 5. Acceptance

Point of truth: [thermal-04](../requirements.md#thermal-04) and [ELEMENT acceptance](../requirements.md#accept-element). Chrome proof through real UI controls is pending the post-catalogue phase.

### Behaviour and numbers

- **Laws.** Supply grant (R2, R3): the plate asks for 120 W and receives what the source allocates. Heat credit (T4): the granted joules of a tick enter `disc`. Exchange (T3): Q̇ = G·ΔT to ambient (0.5 W/K) and to each touching face.
- **Energy.** 120 W ÷ 120 Hz = 1 J per tick, which the delivered Battery pays at its default limit (120 W, `elements/Battery/BatteryElementDeclaration.cs:44`). Its default 3600 J (`:42`) lasts 3600 ÷ 120 = 30 s.
- **Unloaded warm-up.** Time constant C ÷ G = 15.25 ÷ 0.5 = 30.5 s. Final temperature 288 + 120 ÷ 0.5 = 528 K. T(t) = 288 + 240·(1 − e^(−t/30.5)).
  - 330 K (the sensor's default threshold): t = −30.5·ln(1 − 42 ÷ 240) = 30.5 × 0.1924 = 5.9 s, costing 704 J.
  - At 10 s: 288 + 240 × (1 − e^(−0.3279)) = 288 + 240 × 0.2796 = 355.1 K. Stored 15.25 × 67.1 = 1023 J; battery debit 1200 J; to ambient 1200 − 1023 = 177 J.
  - At 30 s (default Battery empty): 288 + 240 × (1 − e^(−0.9836)) = 288 + 240 × 0.626 = 438 K. Stored 2292 J, to ambient 1308 J, total 3600 J.
- **Why 15.25 J/K and 0.5 W/K.** The plate must cross a sensor threshold in a few seconds on a default Battery and must still boil the TH-17 kettle (total loss with the kettle 0.8 W/K, so 120 ÷ 0.8 = 150 K of rise is available against the 85 K needed; chain in [TH-17](TH-17-kettle.md#batch-n-chains)). Boiling needs more energy than a default Battery holds: the kettle reaches 373 K after about 53 s (about 6.4 kJ), so a level that boils water gives the Battery a larger `capacity` (14 400 J, its maximum); on a default Battery the water stops near 345 K.
- **Why 120 W.** It is what a default Battery can pay. The earlier 512 W rating was not attainable from any default source and is withdrawn.
- **Hazard, accepted.** An unloaded plate fed from a larger Battery reaches the tinder's 450 K at t = 30.5 × ln(240 ÷ 78) = 34.3 s (4.1 kJ). A default Battery empties at 438 K, below it. This is a physical consequence a level may use; no cut-out is declared.
- **Shared source.** Two plates on one default Battery get 60 W each: final 288 + 60 ÷ 0.5 = 408 K; at 10 s, 288 + 120 × 0.2796 = 321.5 K.
- **Exhaustion.** Battery `capacity` 600 J: heating stops at 5 s with the disc at 288 + 240 × (1 − e^(−0.1639)) = 324.3 K; 30.5 s later it has cooled to 288 + 36.3 × e^(−1) = 301.4 K.
- **Tick order.** The supply solve grants energy inside tick N's substeps; the thermal step of tick N credits it and exchanges heat; the temperature commits with tick N; a threshold over it acts from tick N + 1 (T8, D7).
- **Run length these rows need: 40 s** (row A3 observes at 35.5 s; ENGINE-RUN-LENGTH, owner decisions 13 and 17; default 30 s, maximum 300 s). Every other row fits in 30 s.
- **Tolerances** (T12): temperature 2 K or 5% of the expected rise, whichever is larger; energy 2% of the transfer or 5 J; stall guard the smaller of 3 × the hand time and the hand time + 60 s, at least 10 s.

## 11. Thermal conventions (owned here; every thermal spec cites this section)

These are the family's input to ENGINE-THERMAL's Code Map. A change is made here, never beside it. The engine has none of this today (R9 to R13). Items settled by owner decisions 19 and 20 and coordinator decision 21 of 22 November 2026 are marked. Repaired on 23 November 2026 against the conventions re-audit (`docs/planning/elements/readiness/audits/conventions-reaudit.md`) and the condensing flag of `docs/planning/elements/readiness/audits/reaudit-group-3.md`, under coordinator decisions 26 and 28 of 22 November 2026: T2, T3, T4, T5, T6, T8, T9, T10, T11 and T13; and in a follow-up pass the same day (coordinator decision 30 and `docs/planning/elements/readiness/audits/reaudit-group-3.md` section 5): T3 (the fusible link's collar on the disc), T4 and T9 (a heat target requests nothing at a limit; only the upper limits refuse heat), T11 (the saturation table stated here, game steam's limits, the expander limit η_max). The tick order is the [one combined order](EL-176-red-laser-emitter.md#combined-tick-order) stated in optical convention 4.

- **T1 Units.** Temperature in kelvin; ambient 288 K. Heat and energy in joules; power in watts; heat capacity in J/K (declared directly, or as mass × specific heat); conductance in W/K; pressure in pascals absolute, ambient 101 325 Pa; durations in seconds of game time. All f32.
- **T2 Thermal node.** A declared lumped store on a body of a package: a local typed id, a heat capacity, an initial temperature (ambient unless declared), an ambient conductance and zero or more *faces* (a collider of that body, or a declared disc aperture of it, with a face conductance). Its state is its enthalpy H in joules; its temperature is a declared function of H (T = H ÷ C away from a phase row, T5). **Every liquid store is also a thermal node with an authored starting temperature (owner decision 20):** its capacity is mass × the liquid's specific heat, inflow mixes by mass, outflow leaves at the store's temperature, and there is no second kind of store. **Heat moves with liquid (coordinator decision 26; the same text as liquid L2):** in the liquid step, per substep, every transfer of volume ΔV carries ΔV × the source store's enthalpy per unit volume H ÷ V; a fitting passes the mixed enthalpy per unit volume of its inflows; a stream credits its enthalpy with its volume; spilled and drained volume carries its enthalpy into that account (evaporated mass carries its own by T5). This reads no temperature and, with liquid L7's bound ΔV ≤ V, never makes H negative. ENGINE-THERMAL adds this column to the liquid step; the thermal step (T8) reads H as the last substep committed it. A gas store is a node through the energy it already carries (Σ mᵢ·Cvᵢ·T over its components, pneumatic P1; R7). A body with no node stores and passes no heat. Node counts are fixed at compile time.
- **T3 Links.** One law for every link: Q̇ = G·(T_a − T_b), from hot to cold.
  - *Internal link*: between two nodes of one package, with a declared G, or a slab G = k·A ÷ L from declared conductivity, area and length.
  - *Contact link (coordinator decision 21)*: exists between two face colliders while their shapes touch or overlap within one declared touch tolerance, which ENGINE-THERMAL states once. Pairs are found through ENGINE-SPATIAL-QUERIES: pairs of static colliders once at Run; pairs with a moving member are added on begin-touch and removed on end-touch (ENGINE-CONTACT-OBSERVATIONS, any body pair). They are held in a link table of compiled capacity that persists while an island sleeps (a requirement this places on ENGINE-ISLANDS-SLEEP, which is planned: no island sleeping exists in the engine today). A link never depends on contact force. G = G_a·G_b ÷ (G_a + G_b). Neither side names a permitted counterpart; a gap wider than the tolerance gives no link. Conducting fixtures are therefore static parts; only cargo is dynamic.
  - *Static-on-static pairs the family uses* (each placed by the player with the two faces touching): bar end, panel, sink foot, thermostat base, ice-plug tab, bladder neck, heat pump, generator and the fusible link's collar (TH-16, its collar's underside set on the disc's top) on the Heating plate's disc (the expansion rod of TH-23 is a moving member on its slider, a begin/end-touch pair); sink foot on a heat pump face or a generator's cold plate; sink foot or heat pump face on the condenser's shelf; freezing-mold tray on a heat pump face; bar end on the absorber's rail, on a panel and on a second bar; panel between any two of these. Each spec's probe cites R10 for its pairs.
  - *Attachment link*: the same series rule across a player-made connection whose two sockets declare a face conductance (connections are ENGINE-ASSEMBLY-JOINTS').
  - *Wetted link*: between a vessel's node and the liquid or gas store it declares it contains; its conductance is the declared value × the store's fill fraction.
  - *Region link*: a bounded box on a frame body, fed by one node, with a declared conductance G_r and a declared axis. A face collider's share is the area of overlap of its bounds with the box, projected on the plane normal to that axis, ÷ the box's area on that plane; shares are granted nearest-first along the axis and their sum is capped at 1. A collider's bounds are its axis-aligned bounds in the box's frame; an overlap of zero thickness grants no share; nearest-first is measured from the box face at the axis origin; a region link grants no share to the faces of the node that feeds it. This is how a flame or a jet heats what is held in it.
  - *Ambient*: every node exchanges G_amb·(T − 288 K) with the world account `ToAmbient`. G_amb is declared (h·A with h = 2.5 W/(m²·K) in still air). A node may declare one forced-air sample point, which excludes its owner's colliders and takes no force; G_amb is then multiplied by (2.5 + u) ÷ 2.5, u being the committed flow speed of the delivered airflow law at that point. In the Vacuum atmosphere preset G_amb is zero.
  - *Radiation*: none between nodes. Radiant heating is absorbed light or radiation, booked once (coordinator decision 26): where a collider is a face of a thermal node with a declared absorptance a, a × the energy the one optical and radiation solve booked on that collider's absorbed record in the tick (optical 7 and 8: collimated power ending on it and its conserved diverging shares; radiation 10: its absorbed power as that item defines it), after any store credit of optical 9 has taken its fraction, moves from that record to the node in the thermal step; the remainder stays on the record. A reading is an observation of flux through an aperture and credits nothing by itself. ENGINE-THERMAL delivers this row as an interface whose end-to-end proof lands in ENGINE-OPTICAL, the first story that books absorbed energy. Glow is presentation only.
- **T4 Sources and sinks.** Every source is a conversion that credits a node and debits a finite store by the same joules in the same tick: electrical (a supply consumer's heat target; credit = the energy granted over the tick's substeps; a heat target whose node is at a declared limit at which it accepts no heat, T9, requests nothing in that tick, so no grant is debited for heat the node would refuse: coordinator decision 30, `ENGINE-THERMAL` with `ENGINE-SUPPLY-DEMAND`, whose grant per tick is power limit × dt), chemical (a fuel store burned at a declared rate, ENGINE-IGNITION-BLAST, its payment credited to declared nodes by declared shares, less any radiant watts paid to an emitter; the combustion family's split is TH-01's section 11), frictional (the work a contact's friction, a joint's friction row or a resisting drive dissipated, summed over all the tick's substeps from each substep's accepted solve only, split between declared nodes by a declared share), optical and radiation (absorbed energy, T3), inventory (a declared finite inventory paid at a constant declared power, debited each tick and restored on Reset: RAD-20's decay heat), and work moved by a declared converter between two nodes (the three flows sum to zero; a declaration whose law can exceed the Carnot fraction is refused). **Heat or light becomes electrical supply in one form only (coordinator decision 21):** the declared fraction of the converted energy is credited to a finite store that an ordinary supply source draws from; a part may gate the source's availability with a threshold node (optical 9). The credited store (a supply source whose finite store has a declared capacity, power limit, initial charge and enable, as constants or bound to parameters, and gains energy during a Run only by a credit) is one row of ENGINE-THERMAL, which lands before ENGINE-OPTICAL (owner decision 18; coordinator decision 26, 22 November 2026; Batch O G20). The receiver credit is ENGINE-OPTICAL's row (optical 9); a thermal converter's credit is ENGINE-THERMAL's. The only unbounded sink is ambient. Nothing creates heat without a paid store.
- **T5 Phase rows.** A node or a liquid store may declare thresholds on its enthalpy curve: melt and freeze at T_m with latent heat L_m × mass; boil and condense at T_sat(p) from the saturation table of the declared "game steam" material row (T11); ignite at T_ign (handing over to ENGINE-IGNITION-BLAST). While a latent amount is being supplied or removed the temperature holds at the threshold and a committed phase fraction moves from 0 to 1. **Boiling conserves energy (owner decision 19):** boiling a mass m moves m·Cp_v·T_sat out of the liquid store's node and into the gas store's vapour component, which is exactly the enthalpy that vapour carries under the gas law; the liquid gives up m·c·T_sat of it and the remainder, m·(Cp_v − c)·T_sat, is the latent heat the heat source must supply. **Condensing has one form, the wall-limited row** (the condenser's, TH-18, adopted here; coordinator decision 26 for the vapour's partial pressure): a condense row is declared on a wetted link (T3) between a gas store holding vapour and the node that takes the latent heat (a vessel wall, or the liquid store's own node), and names the liquid store that receives the condensate. While that node is below the dew point T_dew, the saturation temperature of the vapour's partial pressure (pneumatic P1) on the game-steam table, clamped at the table's ends, vapour moves from the gas store to the liquid store at ṁ = G × (T_dew − T_node) ÷ L_v(T_dew), G being the link's declared conductance, never more than the vapour present. The row moves m·Cp_v·T_dew out of the gas store: m·c·T_dew enters the liquid store with the liquid and the latent m·L_v(T_dew) enters the node, so it conserves exactly as boiling does. The link's sensible exchange G·(T_gas − T_node) runs beside it under T3. The kettle's condense direction is this row on its own wetted link (TH-17), the condenser's is the same row on its wall (TH-18), each declaring it in these words with its own store, node and G, and there is no other condensing rule. What a change does to a body is declared from general capabilities: mass moved to or from a liquid store (ENGINE-LIQUID) or a gas store (ENGINE-GAS-NETWORK); a collider and its art active over a declared interval of a committed phase scalar (the phase fraction, or the solid amount, fraction × the store's volume, for a store whose fill varies), and a body's mass frame recomputed from its active colliders at a tick boundary (both ENGINE-THERMAL's; a collider that becomes inactive wakes the islands it supported); a seat opening driven by the phase fraction (liquid convention L14(d)); a load limit that falls with temperature (ENGINE-FRACTURE). Mass is conserved across each change. A phase row moves mass between a liquid store and a gas store of one package without a flow edge; any other meeting of gas and liquid is a declared edge of ENGINE-LIQUID with its own head cap, which debits the gas store for the work it does (liquid L15; coordinator decisions 16 and 26). **Evaporation** is a phase row on a liquid store with a free surface: mass leaves at a declared rate × (1 − φ) × the airflow multiplier of T3 (of the node the row is declared on), carrying its own enthalpy m·c·T out of the store, while m·L_v(T) (T11) is taken from the declared node (the store's own, or a node wetted by it, such as a wick); both go to the world account `Evaporated`, which liquid L3 and L10 count in volume (coordinator decision 26); φ is the scene relative humidity, one validated level value from 0 to 1, default 0.5, owned by ENGINE-THERMAL with this row. In the Vacuum preset the row is off.
- **T6 Expansion.** A declared binding from a node's temperature to the rest position of one prismatic or revolute coordinate: x₀ = k·(T − T_ref), realised by the coordinate's position drive with a declared stiffness and a declared force or torque limit, so a blocked part builds load and does not teleport. The drive's positive work in a tick is debited from the node and negative work credited (T13). A gas store expands by its own gas law. **Lift in air is a binding of this kind, not a displaced-air law (coordinator decision 28):** a body in air declares its ambient lift, and a heated envelope's declared lift has its magnitude bound to the envelope node's committed temperature, lift = declared envelope volume × ambient air density × g × (1 − T_ambient ÷ T_envelope), evaluated each tick; the law is ENGINE-THERMAL's and the binding mechanism ENGINE-PARAMETER-BOUND-FRAMES'. Buoyancy in liquid stays liquid L12's displaced-volume law.

- **T8 Tick phase.** The thermal step's slot is the [one combined tick order](EL-176-red-laser-emitter.md#combined-tick-order) (optical convention 4; coordinator decisions 16 and 26), which this item does not restate: one thermal step per committed tick of duration dt, after the last mechanical substep and the optical and radiation solve that follows it, and before sensor evaluation and commit. It reads this tick's supply grants, the friction and drive work summed over the tick's substeps (each substep's accepted solve only), the touching face pairs as of the last substep, the absorbed records this tick's solve has just booked (T3), and each liquid store's enthalpy as the liquid step's last substep committed it: heat carried by liquid flow moves in the liquid step, per substep (T2), never here. - **T9 Stability (coordinator decision 21).** Each link moves, in compiled link order and from current temperatures, ΔQ = ΔT · C_r · (1 − e^(−G·dt ÷ C_r)), with C_r = C_a·C_b ÷ (C_a + C_b) (C_r = C for a link to ambient or to a held temperature). This is the exact two-node solution: one rule, conserving, monotone and stable for any G and C, so no declaration is refused for stiffness. A node, liquid store or phase mass whose capacity is zero is inactive and its links move nothing. A node holding at a phase threshold (T5) counts as a held temperature, so its links use C_r = the other node's C. A gas store at its material's upper temperature limit or its pressure limit (pneumatic P14) accepts no heat: a link or source that would raise it moves nothing in that direction, and the paying node or store keeps its energy (coordinator decision 26); an electrical heat target on it requests nothing in that tick (T4; coordinator decision 30), and a boil row (T5) into a gas store at its pressure limit moves nothing in that tick, so no store is boiled past its limit (the liquid then warms by its own capacity). Heat leaving such a store, and any heat into a store at its lower temperature limit, moves as usual. The family needs this: its smallest nodes are 0.004 J/K against faces of 16 W/K, for which G·dt ÷ C is about 16 to 25, far above the limit of an explicit step.

- **T11 Game scale (D5).** Condensed-phase specific heat is SI × ¼: water 1046, ice 525, steel 122, copper 96, brass 95, aluminium 225, ceramic 210, wood 425, wax 625, gel 875, toast 700 J/(kg·K). Latent heat of fusion is SI × 1/16: water 20 875 J/kg at 273 K; wax 12 500 J/kg at 330 K. **Boiling (owner decision 19):** game steam is a declared gas with R 461.5 (SI), Cv 691.7 and Cp_v 1153.2 J/(kg·K) (γ = 1.667 ≈ 5/3, the monatomic limit), so the latent heat of vaporisation is L_v(T) = (Cp_v − c)·T = 107.2 J/(kg·K) × T: **40 000 J/kg at 373 K and 101 325 Pa** (30 900 J/kg at 288 K for evaporation). **The saturation table is part of the declared "game steam" material row**, shared material data that TH-17 to TH-20 and TH-35 read and no element owns: T_sat(p), linear between the points (20 000 Pa, 333.2 K), (50 000, 354.5), (101 325, 373.0), (150 000, 384.5), (200 000, 393.4), (300 000, 406.7), (400 000, 416.8), (600 000, 432.0), (800 000, 443.6) (real water's values), clamped below 20 000 Pa at 333.2 K, so a wall colder than 333.2 K condenses any vapour present. Game steam's limits are Air's (pneumatic P14): 150 K to 600 K, at most 10 kg/m³ and 800 000 Pa; the table therefore spans every pressure a store admits (a partial pressure never exceeds its store's total) and is never read above its top. This one declared material value (Cp_v, with c and R) is the whole of boiling, condensing and evaporating; it is stated here only. Why this value: it is the smallest latent heat for which the vapour is still a valid ideal gas with the SI gas constant (γ = 5/3 is the upper limit; a smaller value would need γ above it or a changed R), and it gives a playable rate: a kettle on the 120 W plate has 50.8 W to spare at the boil (TH-17), which bounds the steam at 50.8 ÷ 40 000 = 1.27 g/s, 0.0022 m³/s at ambient pressure (the default kettle makes 1.17 g/s at its default fill, less as it empties, because the wetted link scales with fill and the head takes its share), and never more than 120 ÷ 40 000 = 3.0 g/s. Because Cv < c, vapour made into a rigid head returns (c − Cv)·T = 354.3 × T J/kg to the vessel; this is self-limiting (T_sat rises about four times faster than the vessel warms) and conserving. Game steam is not Clausius–Clapeyron consistent with the saturation table (the latent heat is far below real water's while the table keeps real water's slope), so an isentropic expansion can yield more work per kilogram than the latent heat paid. **Expander limit, checkable from one declaration:** the game-steam row also declares the largest efficiency an expanding work target (TH-20) may declare, η_max = 0.25, and bundle validation refuses with a typed reason any expander whose declared efficiency exceeds it; nothing about the scene or the source is read. Why 0.25: a kilogram of vapour made from water at 288 K and expanded from saturation at pressure p to ambient gives at best η × Cp_v × T_sat × (1 − (101 325 ÷ p)^0.4) of shaft work against L_v(T_sat) + c × (T_sat − 288) of heat paid, and that ratio must stay below the Carnot fraction 1 − 288 ÷ T_sat at every point of the table above ambient. The largest η meeting this falls as p rises (0.554 at 150 000 Pa, 0.273 at 400 000 Pa, 0.256 at 800 000 Pa) and is least, 0.2561, near 743 000 Pa on the table's interpolation; at 0.25 the ratio is 0.177 against 0.268 at 200 000 Pa, 0.283 against 0.309 at 400 000 Pa and 0.342 against 0.351 at 800 000 Pa. Superheat only lowers the ratio: at a given pressure each further joule of superheat yields at most η × (1 − (101 325 ÷ p)^0.4) of work, which is below that pressure's saturated ratio because Cp_v × T_sat exceeds the heat paid per kilogram (443 405 J against 142 157 J at 150 000 Pa; 511 560 J against 210 312 J at 800 000 Pa), while the Carnot fraction rises with the inlet temperature. No store exceeds 800 000 Pa (a boil row into a store at that limit moves nothing, T9), so the table covers every inlet state and the check bounds them all. A change to the table or to Cp_v, c or R re-derives η_max here. The earlier 141 062 J/kg is withdrawn. Fuel energy is SI × 1/1024: wax 41 016 J/kg, wood and fibre 15 625 J/kg. Liquid water is 16 kg/m³ (D2): 1 kg is 0.0625 m³. Face conductance: metal 64, ceramic, wood, ice and soft faces 16, a probe tip 0.5 W/K. Ignition: fibre 450 K, wax wick 500 K, wood 573 K.
