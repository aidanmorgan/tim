## Design candidates — pipes, electronics and optics (subagent research, 27 September 2026)

These are researched puzzle options; checked entries below record implemented features, while unchecked entries remain pending. They are not claims about the original TIM editions. Three research subagents considered transport, complementary mechanisms and beginner-friendly 3D interaction; follow-up research covers timers and optics.
