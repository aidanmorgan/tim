### Accepted-work convention

Reuse existing motor-source RevoluteStateRead.Work and its documented convention; do not introduce another motor ledger or battery debit. New per-transmission work is signed work of accepted solver impulses, not unique stored-joule provenance.

**Window-midpoint rule (Review repair 3, Q15; replaces the former per-application midpoint).** A work window is the fixed-pose interval from one position integration (`IntegrateBodies`) to the next (amended by Q16 planning check 1; the earlier per-pass split was finer than the fixed pose requires). One window therefore holds, in order: relax(k), restitution(k) and its energy clamp, contact-work store impulses(k), membrane(k+1), and warm start plus every solve iteration of substep k+1. Its start and end rates are consecutive integration velocities — the velocities that actually move the body.

Inside a window, these velocity changes are non-channel impulses: gravity (observed separately by displacement), drag, guide application and `SettleVelocity`/`ClampVelocity`. They are not credited to contact or interface work.

The window that is open at a tick commit is carried across the tick as committed state: each channel's accumulated impulse and the window start rates. The carried state is owned like every other committed field: candidate and trial capture/restore, staged discard, Reset and installation generation. Reset and admission start with an empty window at the admitted velocity. A motor-budget trial restore resets the open accumulators and the window's start rates to their values at trial start, together with the rest of the trial state. It discards the window the failed trial opened at its `IntegrateBodies` and reinstates the pre-trial window.

Why integration-to-integration: a relax-end velocity is never integrated, and the next solve cancels it. A window ending there charged a contact that merely holds a load about −4e-5 J per substep, a drift that grows with time. With integration-velocity endpoints a steady hold credits zero when its integration velocities are zero; under rest jitter it stays within the duration-scaled allowance (Q18).

Within a window, a channel accumulates its complete applied impulse; it is credited only once, when the window closes, against the window's own start and end rates:
- winding interface, per coordinate contribution with coefficient a and accumulated row impulse Λ: W = a·Λ·(rate_start+rate_end)/2;
- contact on the retained moving body, with accumulated linear impulse P and angular impulse L about the centre of mass from every normal, friction, rolling and spin application: W = P·(v_start+v_end)/2 + L·(ω_start+ω_end)/2.

Rates are the body's actual linear/angular rates, including guided components and moving-frame angular terms. Freeze the actual coordinate Jacobian per application and merge repeated bodies as before. Never apply endpoints independently to measure them.

Why: crediting each application at its own before/after midpoint splits one body's kinetic-energy change between rows by solve order. A contact that restores velocity a spring row just removed was charged −F²dt²/2m per substep, so a contact merely holding a spring load reported negative work that grew with time. Under the window rule, a body's channels plus the window's non-channel velocity changes (each expressed as an impulse m·Δv credited at the same window midpoint) sum exactly to its window ΔKE. That holds for the angular part too, because each window has a fixed pose and therefore fixed world inertia; the credit uses that window's world inertia. Windows are contiguous at integration instants, so credits add across windows. The tight positive control (ContactWork ≈ head KE within 0.005 J at k = .001) is valid only in its gravity-free, drag-free scene. A contact holding a body whose integration velocities are zero earns no work; under solver rest jitter (sub-`RestSpeed` velocities of a soft contact under load) the reading stays within the duration-scaled work allowance of 0.005 J per second of hold (Q18). Order cannot move work between channels.

The restitution clamp scales only the restitution pass's own impulse contribution (its impulses above the relaxed impulses) within the open window's accumulators, by the same factor s it applies to the velocity change: each body's accumulators change by −(1 − s)·P_restitution, linear and angular. It does not scale the window's other impulses or subtract kinetic energy separately.

Warm start and signed corrective impulses are inside the window sum. Only after a window's signed sum is complete may explicitly named positive transfer accumulate max(0,W_window); per-iteration positive clamping would invent work. Clear disabled relation warm starts before application. Energy-removing contacts are included. Gravity work is m*g·accepted COM displacement.
