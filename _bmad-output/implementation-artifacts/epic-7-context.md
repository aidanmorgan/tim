Delete obsolete paths forward without compatibility shims, legacy modes or fallback implementations. Keep current typed declarations, f32 numerical authority, general engines and the Tri-Graph architecture. Detailed device/performance qualification remains at its named later gates; no scoped purge claims global qualification.

## Technical Decisions

Use the accepted Story 7.0 specifications and baseline-pinned harvest as implementation knowledge after sources are deleted. The ledger distinguishes element facts from obsolete mechanisms. Archive verification must precede irreversible loss of untracked files. Current documents carry intended behavior; old proof is reusable only when its original identity and applicability are independently established.
