## Design Notes

**Selected technical design (coordinator, after independent read-only investigation by `/root/next_spec_draft`).** Use exact oriented-normal friction patches, one shared law for every patch. This is geometric partitioning, not a planar-versus-hollow mode. Keep the original multi-normal manifold for normal/restitution coupling and rolling. No contact is discarded and no average normal can cancel.

Build patches after internal-edge removal. Group equal finite normal components (signed zero equal), canonical order by normal coordinates; order members by world contact coordinates then feature identity. Shared planar/line generators supply identical normals, so they obtain one central anchor. Distinct curved/opposing normals remain distinct patches. Canonical order removes input-enumeration dependence; do not claim mathematically simultaneous solving of different patches.

For each patch, use its unweighted contact centroid and RMS tangent distance `r` from that centroid. Geometry, not changing normal-load weights, determines anchor/radius. Capacity is `μ N`, `μ=sqrt(μA μB)`, `N=Σ max(λN,0)` **within that patch only**. Use two central tangent Jacobians and scaled normal-axis twist row `r·ωrelative`; the third impulse is `τ/r`. Point radius zero gives a zero twist Jacobian naturally. The single admissible budget is `λ1²+λ2²+λ3² ≤ (μN)²`, preventing separate force/twist rows from spending full capacity twice.

Use the full symmetric effective-mass coupling `K=J M⁻¹ Jᵀ`, not three diagonal masses. Minimize the accumulated-impulse kinetic quadratic with free row velocity `Jv−Kλold` under that budget. Choose one bounded deterministic f32 PSD numerical method; document its stopping/rank treatment from arithmetic, not observed passing output. Rank-zero twist and singular recovery must solve this same quadratic, never dispatch to old per-point friction. Projecting an unconstrained solution radially is not generally the constrained minimum for coupled K. Do not add an ad hoc per-delta energy clamp: undoing an accumulated impulse can increase current energy while still minimizing relative to the zero-friction state. Preserve existing whole-contact energy acceptance.

Normal warm starts stay point-feature based. Patch warm starts use exact canonical feature membership plus collider pair (typed key), world-space tangent impulse and normal-axis torque, reprojected into current geometry/budget and applied once.
