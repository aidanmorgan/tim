Results (drop-landing-axial-creep.log, drop ramp): (1) and (4) both land with the axis tilted by -1.47e-4 rad in y and, once
stopped, creep along the axis at ~5e-5 m/s indefinitely (body velocity read constant, e.g. v_y -1.1e-4, v_z -1.05e-5); the Weight
moved 0.497 mm along its axis in 14 s. (2) shows no creep at rest or after a bench roll (< 0.02 mm in 14 s); (3) the base-origin
Weight resting on its side does not move at all. So the creep is the engine's (drop landing onto the side), not the package's
offset centre of mass. With the flush ramp the same roll moves 0.016 mm along its axis in total and nothing after stopping.
