## Intent

**Problem:** Eight delivered parts break the owner's rule that what a player sees as solid is solid, or that a drawn solid sits on the part it belongs to.
- Raised marks: the Clutch's two shaft spokes stand 35 mm proud of their spinning faces; the Reverse gear's two index bars 40 mm and two gold marks 100 mm; the Motor's rotor bar 4 mm. The Clutch also draws a solid axle with no collider.
- The Counter's foot, face panel and three sockets hang 5 to 30 mm clear of its housing.
- Floating art that parts B and C leave as drawn (coordinator decision 39): the Linear pusher's two upper sockets (7.6 cm off the barrel); the Wound spring's activation socket, shaft wheel and latch lever (0.35 to 0.39 m in front of its rails); the Cannon's charge track (4.5 cm above the foot) and two sockets (3 cm above the foot); the Windmill's output wheel (0.105 m in front of the nacelle).
- Floating solids found by the fifth audit (coordinator decision 42, 24 November 2026): the Linear pusher's barrel (0.075 m above its foot), the Wound spring's sleeve (0.045 m from each rail), the Clutch's two shafts (0.04 m in front of their blocks), the Motor's rotor (0.045 m) and the Reverse gear's two wheels (0.08 m) in front of their housings.

**Approach:** Scene and declaration data only. Print the spokes, index bars, marks and rotor bar flat on their faces (a mark on a spinning body must not strike cargo the player did not build), give the Clutch axle a static cylinder collider of its drawn size, and seat every floating solid on the solid it belongs to, moving each art node, its hidden proxy (parts B and C), its port locator and declared port together (for a turning body also its hinge frame and rim). The Counter's seats are decided in CAT-020 repair 3; the others are decided below (Seated centres) and computed from their scenes.
