## Intent

**Problem:** Struck balls never stop. The worker never reads the declared `LinearDrag` (body record byte 66), and contact friction is pure Coulomb, so a sphere rolling without slipping feels no opposing force. Even if linear drag 0.04 were applied it would take about two minutes to stop a 0.7 m/s ball. The CAT-014 acceptance "both balls rest without jitter" therefore cannot hold.

**Approach:** Apply each dynamic body's declared linear drag per substep, and add a declared rolling-resistance coefficient to the ball material that the generic contact solver applies as a torque opposing rolling at a sphere's support contact, clamped so it brings the ball to rest and never reverses it. Both are declaration data read from the compiled records; no element identifiers, no hidden constants, no sleep hack.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- Linear drag: `v *= exp(-drag*dt)` per substep right after gravity, from body record +66; Static bodies stay drag 0.
- Rolling resistance: a new declared material field (Half, coefficient Crr, bounded e.g. [0, 0.1]) carried by `BallMaterial` per kind (Basketball and Bowling values chosen in the physically plausible range so a struck ball at ≤ 1 m/s comes to rest within ~5 s on the bench) and compiled into the physics material record (use free bytes; if the material record layout changes, update `PhysicsGpuAbi`, worker offsets and the harness ByteLength together). Applied in the worker as an angular impulse opposing the relative rolling at each sphere contact with magnitude ≤ Crr·λN·r per substep and clamped so it cannot reverse ω; boxes/planes/walls declare 0.
- Owner decision 2026-10-09: rolling resistance applies at EVERY sphere contact (static or dynamic partner), with the equal and opposite reaction on the partner; Domino outcomes may change as a physical consequence and Story 6.1's two-lane demo is re-tuned to still separate the balls.
- Owner decision 2026-10-09: Bowling ball becomes smaller and heavier than the Basketball — radius 0.28 m, mass 4 kg (bounce 0.14, drag 0.04 unchanged); the Story 6.1 two-lane geometry and its harness/e2e margins are re-tuned for the new size under every-contact rolling resistance (Bowling target tilt > 60°, Basketball < 20°, both balls rest).
- Rolling-resistance decrements must survive the per-tick commit at 60/120/240 Hz (harness fact per cadence). Owner decision 2026-10-09: survival of the declared linear drag (0.04) in free flight at every cadence is delivered by Story 6.1c (f32 committed velocity), not by this story.
- Restore the CAT-014 frozen row: e2e and harness assert both balls come to rest (speed below a stated threshold, no jitter over 1 s) after the two-lane strike.
- The rolling-resistance coefficient is derived from the ball kind (`BallMaterial.For(kind)`), like friction and bounce threshold, and is not persisted in the save wire (ball slot bytes 114–115 stay zero padding, so pre-6.1b saves load); keep validation default-bits-only per kind (coordinator amendment 2026-10-09, see Spec Change Log); update docs (`general-engine-design.md:81`, game-grade envelope) and deferred-work (close ENGINE-DRAG).
- Re-run and pass every prior suite serially in Chrome; first-principles ramp solve and Receiver capture must still succeed.

**Never:**
- A rest/sleep threshold that zeroes velocity, angular drag that damps free-flight spin, or per-element branches. Boxes and planes declare rolling resistance 0 (Domino outcomes may still change through the ball's resistance at their contacts — owner decision above).
